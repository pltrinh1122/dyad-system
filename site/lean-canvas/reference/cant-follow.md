F0 [symptom; H] It gave me something I can't follow, so I can't stand behind it.
  observed: I read the code, analysis, document or plan it produced and cannot say in my own words why it is the way it is; seen at the end of a session after one large prompt, during a session after a series of prompts, and on resuming a session left earlier
  F1 [cause; H] It arrived as one finished whole, larger than I can take in in the time I have.
    observed: during a session, after one prompt that asked for the whole thing; I get many pages or files at once and skim
    F1.1 [root-cause; H] I meet the result all at once, after it is made, with no point along the way where my understanding had to keep up with it.
      observed: I approved nothing between asking and receiving; understanding it is a separate job that starts only when the work is over
      removal test [H]: if my understanding had to keep pace step by step, F1 and F6 fall away and F0 shrinks to the parts beyond my competence (F3)
  F2 [cause; H] I cannot find, for any part of it, a reason I could repeat to someone else.
    observed: during or at the end of a session; the text states what is so, and no part says why it was chosen over another way
    F2.1 [root-cause; H] I am handed conclusions without the decisions behind them, so I cannot tell what it chose, what it assumed and what it guessed.
      observed: what it considered and dropped is gone once it answers; every sentence sounds equally sure
      removal test [H]: if every decision, assumption and guess were shown as such, F2, F4 and F5 fall away, and F0 remains only where the volume (F1) or the domain (F3) is beyond me
  F3 [cause; H] It works in a field or at a level above my own, because that is why I asked for it.
    observed: new session, after one prompt in a domain I do not know; the wording and the moves are fluent and unfamiliar
    F3.1 [root-cause; H] I asked for help where I am weakest, so the result lands exactly where I am least able to judge it.
      observed: the parts I cannot follow are the parts I could not have done myself
      removal test [H]: if the result reached me at the level I can judge, F3 falls away; F1 and F2 remain for a cursory user, so F0 shrinks but does not vanish
  F4 [cause; H] I cannot tell whether I fail to follow it or it is not what I asked for.
    observed: after one prompt, new session; it never said back what it took me to mean before it started, so my confusion has two possible sources
    → F2.1
  F5 [cause; H] When I ask it to explain, cite or plan first, the answer is as hard to follow as the work, or does not match what it then did.
    observed: intermediate user, during a session after a series of prompts; the plan given first and the result delivered differ, and the citations do not say which claim rests on which
    → F2.1
  F6 [cause; H] Over a series of prompts, or on coming back to a session, I lost track of what was settled and what changed.
    observed: resumed session, after a series; I am looking at a version whose earlier steps I no longer hold
    → F1.1
  F7 [cause; H] Why must I be able to follow it at all? Because someone will ask me why it is this way, and "the assistant did it" is not an answer I can give.
    observed: before handing it on to a reviewer, client or manager, I picture being asked and having no answer of my own
    F7.1 [anchor; H] I answer for the result.
