X0 [symptom; H] The AI states something false as if it were true.
  observed: a colleague or reader points out the error; a fact contradicts what you know; a link or a name it gave does not exist; usually late, after you relied on it.
  X1 [cause; H] It produces what is likely, not what is checked: a plausible answer and a true one look the same to it.
    X1.1 [cause; H] Nothing between the AI and you compares the claim with a source.
      X1.1.1 [root-cause; H] Nothing checks what the AI produced against what is true, or what you wanted, before you rely on it.
        removal test [H]: if each claim were checked before you relied on it, X0 falls away for X1, X4 and X5; X2 and X3 remain.
  X2 [cause; H] It sounds just as sure when it is wrong.
    observed: confident and wrong; challenged, it flips.
    X2.1 [cause; H] It does not show which parts it knows, which it inferred and which it filled in.
      X2.1.1 [root-cause; H] Nothing shows where an answer came from or how sure it is.
        removal test [H]: if each answer showed its source and how sure it is, X2 and X3 fall away and X0 shrinks; X4 remains.
  X3 [cause; H] It answers past what it knows (missing, out of date, or never given to it) instead of saying so.
    → X2.1.1
  X4 [cause; H] It answered a different question from the one you meant.
    → X1.1.1
  X5 [cause; H] You could not tell, because checking would cost more than asking did.
    → X1.1.1
  X6 [cause; H] Why does it matter at all? You pass the answer on, and you answer for it.
    → X1.1.1
    → X2.1.1
    X6.1 [anchor; H] I answer for the result.
