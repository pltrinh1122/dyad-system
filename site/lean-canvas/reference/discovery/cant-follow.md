# Discovery: "It gave me something I can't follow, so I can't stand behind it."

Every statement below is a hypothesis (H). Users: cursory (assistant out of the box) to intermediate (asks it to ask back, cite, or plan first).

## Anchor (Step 1)
When I let an agent produce code, an analysis, a document or a plan that I will hand on under my own name, I want to trust it enough to answer for the result, so that what I stand behind is something I could explain and defend myself. Root anchor: I answer for the result.

## Divergence by lens (Step 2; none judged here)
- visibility
  - [H] I see the finished thing, not the steps that made it.
  - [H] What it looked at, tried and dropped is not in front of me.
  - [H] After a series of prompts I cannot see which parts changed and which stayed.
- verifiability
  - [H] I have no way to check a part except by redoing it.
  - [H] The sources it cites are many, or do not say which sentence rests on which.
  - [H] When I ask it to explain, the explanation is more of its text, as hard to check as the work.
- predictability
  - [H] The plan it gave first does not match what it then delivered, so the plan does not help me read the result.
  - [H] Asked again, it explains the same thing differently, so I do not know which account is the real one.
- reversibility
  - [H] I cannot take out the one part I do not understand without breaking the rest.
  - [H] It is already woven into my work, so backing out means losing what I did understand.
- attribution
  - [H] I cannot tell which choices were mine, which it made, and which it guessed.
  - [H] If asked "who decided this?", I could only say "the tool did".
- competence
  - [H] I asked for help exactly where I am weakest, so the result lands where I cannot judge it.
  - [H] It writes with a fluency and jargon above my level, which reads as expertise I cannot test.
- intent
  - [H] I cannot tell whether I fail to follow it or it is not what I asked for.
  - [H] It never said back what it took me to mean before it started.
- attention
  - [H] It is longer than I have time to read closely.
  - [H] It came all at once, so understanding it is a separate job I had not planned for.
  - [H] When I resume a session, I have lost what was settled in the earlier turns.
- consequence
  - [H] A part I skimmed may be the part that breaks.
  - [H] What I cannot follow I also cannot maintain or extend later.
- accountability to others
  - [H] A reviewer, client or manager will ask me "why this?", and I will have no answer of my own.
  - [H] Saying "the assistant wrote it" does not count as an answer where I work.
- other
  - [H] Asking it to make it simpler feels like admitting I could not do the work.
  - [H] It sounds confident everywhere, so I cannot tell where it is unsure.
