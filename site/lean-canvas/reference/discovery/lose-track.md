# o11 discovery: "With many sessions running, I lose track of what each one is doing."

## Anchor [H]
When I let several agents act at once (edit files, run commands, send things) on work I answer for, I want to trust
each of them enough to answer for the result, so that I can run them side by side without each one needing my eye.

## Divergence by lens (none judged yet; every statement H)
- visibility
  - [H] Each session shows what it did only inside its own window; there is no view of all of them at once.
  - [H] What a session changed is buried in a long scroll of narration, so I see activity, not outcome.
  - [H] A session working and a session stopped look much the same from outside.
- verifiability
  - [H] To check one session's claim of "done" I have to reread its history, and I cannot afford that for every one.
  - [H] Two sessions report success on work that overlaps, and I cannot tell which result is the true one.
- predictability
  - [H] A session I started for one fix widens its scope and starts sub-tasks I did not ask for.
  - [H] The same standing instructions are read differently by different sessions.
- reversibility
  - [H] By the time I notice a session went the wrong way, its changes are mixed with another's and hard to undo alone.
  - [H] I do not know which session made a change, so I do not know what to roll back.
- attribution
  - [H] When something lands, I cannot say which session did it, under which of my instructions.
  - [H] I cannot tell whether an act was something I authorized or something a session decided.
- competence
  - [H] I give each session a domain, then forget which session holds which domain.
  - [H] A session can be strong in its own task and blind to what a neighbouring session is changing.
- intent
  - [H] What I meant when I started a session fades from my memory faster than the session runs.
  - [H] A session keeps going on an old reading of my request after I have changed my mind elsewhere.
- attention
  - [H] I switch between windows and each switch costs me the context I had built for the last one.
  - [H] Questions sessions ask me wait in windows I am not looking at, so work stalls unseen.
- consequence
  - [H] Two sessions write to the same files, branch or outbox, and one silently undoes the other.
  - [H] A session sends something outward while I believed it was still drafting.
- accountability to others
  - [H] A teammate or reviewer asks me what changed today and I cannot answer without digging.
  - [H] I am asked why something went out and I cannot show who decided it.
- other
  - [H] I run more sessions than I can hold in mind because each one alone looked cheap to start.
  - [H] Sessions end or restart and lose their own account of what they were doing.
