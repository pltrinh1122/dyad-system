U0 [symptom; H] It follows my rules sometimes and not others, so I can't rely on it.
  observed: a rule written once is kept in one act and ignored in the next; seen in every session moment (new, during, resumed) and most often after a series of prompts rather than after one
  U1 [cause; H] The same rule holds in one session and lapses in another, and holds early in a session but fades later.
    observed: a new session keeps the rule after one prompt; the same session, deep into a series or resumed, drops it; a parallel session never seemed to have it
    U1.1 [cause; H] Each session carries my rules differently: loaded fresh in one, pushed aside in a long or resumed one, a different copy in another running at the same time.
      observed: after a resume or a long series it acts as if the rule were never written; two sessions started together behave differently on the same rule
      U1.1.1 [root-cause; H] I can't see which of my rules a session is holding at the moment it acts.
        removal test [H]: if I could see the rules in force before each act, U1's surprise variance falls away (I would catch the lapse before the act), and U4 loses its gap (the act would be tied to the rules held); U0 shrinks to the cases U2 and U3 explain
  U2 [cause; H] It treats a rule as one thing to weigh, not a line it will not cross.
    observed: during a session, after one prompt that pulls against a rule, it does what the prompt asks and the rule gives way
    U2.1 [cause; H] When a rule meets the request in front of it, or another of my rules, it settles the clash itself, and differently each time.
      observed: during a series, two of my rules point different ways and it picks one, then the other on a later turn
      U2.1.1 [root-cause; H] My rules collide in cases I never pictured, and each collision is settled out of my sight.
        removal test [H]: if every collision came back to me instead of being settled silently, U2.1 falls away and most of U2's give-way cases with it; U0's "sometimes" loses the share that clashes cause
    U2.2 [cause; H] In a case I did not foresee it reads my words differently from what I meant.
      observed: in a new session after one prompt it keeps the letter of a rule and misses its point, or stretches it to a case I never meant
      U2.2.1 [root-cause; H] I can't tell from my own wording how it will read a rule in a case I didn't picture.
        removal test [H]: if I could know its reading before it acted, U2.2's misreadings fall away and the rest of U2; U0 loses the cases where it "followed" the rule wrongly
  U3 [cause; H] I learn of a lapse only after the act is done.
    observed: during or after a series, I find a changed file, a run command or a sent message that broke a rule, hours later
    U3.1 [cause; H] A skipped rule leaves no mark: it does not say it skipped it, and the result looks the same.
      observed: the session reports success; nothing in its reply shows the rule was not applied
      U3.1.1 [root-cause; H] A broken rule looks just like a kept one until I check the result myself.
        removal test [H]: if a lapse showed itself, U3.1 falls away and U3 becomes "I learn at once"; U0's distrust of the acts I did not check shrinks to U3.2
    U3.2 [cause; H] I can't check every act: several sessions act at once, faster than I can review.
      observed: with several sessions running, acts land while I am reading another session's output
      U3.2.1 [root-cause; H] Every act I don't check myself is one I answer for blind.
        removal test [H]: if an unchecked act were not blind, U3.2 falls away and U3.3 loses its sting; U0's "can't rely on it" loses the acts I never saw
    U3.3 [cause; H] Some of its acts cannot be taken back once done: sent, run, deleted.
      observed: a message sent or a command run against a rule, found after, with nothing to undo
      → U3.2.1
  U4 [cause; H] When a rule was broken I can't show anyone what rule was set, or why it was not kept.
    observed: resumed later or asked by someone else, I can't say whether the rule was in force when the act was taken
    U4.1 [cause; H] Nothing ties an act to the rules the session held when it took it.
      observed: the act and my rules sit in different places; nothing records which of them the session had at that moment
      → U1.1.1
  U5 [cause; H] Why must it follow my rules at all? Because my rules are how my judgment reaches acts I do not watch.
    observed: I write standing rules exactly for the acts I will not be present for: several sessions, unattended runs, messages sent in my name
    U5.1 [anchor; H] I answer for the result.
