V0 [symptom; H] Checking what it gave me takes about as long as doing the work myself, sometimes longer.
  V0.1 [cause; H] I feel I must check all of it myself rather than lean on it, because once I rely on it or pass it on, it is mine.
    V0.1.1 [anchor; H] I answer for the result: whoever receives what I rely on or pass on holds me to it, not the agent.
    → V0.2.1
  V0.2 [cause; H] The only way I have to check it is to do the work again.
    V0.2.1 [root-cause; H] It gives me its conclusions without their grounds — where each claim came from, what it assumed, what it did and did not look at — so its word is all I have, and I cannot check a claim without rebuilding it.
      removal test [H]: with the grounds in front of me, checking a claim becomes looking it up rather than redoing it (V0), a false, stale or invented claim shows a missing or mismatched source (V1), and an answer I could not follow can be followed step by step (V2); of the symptoms whose branches reach it (V0, V1, V2), all three are removed or mostly removed; its next why is V0.1.1.
  V0.3 [cause; H] I check every part with the same care, because I cannot tell which parts need it.
    V0.3.1 [root-cause; H] It sounds just as sure when it is wrong as when it is right, so the way it reads tells me nothing about where it is weak.
      removal test [H]: if how sure it sounds followed how likely it is to be right, I could check the weak parts and lean on the rest (V0, V3), and a wrong part would no longer slip through by looking right (V1); a misread question would show only where it says it was unsure of what I meant (V4, partly); of the symptoms whose branches reach it (V0, V1, V3, V4), three are removed and one partly; its next why is V0.1.1.
    V0.3.2 [cause; H] I do not know how much rides on each part, so I cannot put my checking where a mistake would cost most.
      V0.3.2.1 [root-cause; H] What the result is for — the question it must answer, who has to read it and stand behind it, and what a mistake in it would cost — is never settled between me and the agent before it starts.
        removal test [H]: with that settled first, it answers the question I meant or says which reading it took (V4), I know which parts carry the cost and check those (V3), it writes at the length and level of the person who must stand behind it (V2), and what I must check shrinks to what matters (V0, partly); of the symptoms whose branches reach it (V0, V2, V3, V4), three are removed and one partly; its next why is V0.1.1.
  V0.4 [cause; H] It gives me far more than I asked for or can read with care, faster than I can read it.
    → V0.3.2.1
