## Survivor (the stack, restated)
A seat is as #295 says, and its record holds seat, holder and host. Traffic is #253's three planes.
A cos touches one of them, the announcement plane, in two kinds: it **announces** (undirected; today by
state on `main` and, in the store's absence, by a directed notice that obliges nothing and has no
corpus record) and it **relays** — publishes an Operator's verbatim prompt as an announcement whose
content is the Operator's, which opens a row within one system and is an intake across systems. The
command plane (dispositions) has no carrier and no seat is on it for another seat's rows: every
plan-`Y` and Done-`Y` is asked and given in the session that owns the row. Hand-carry is not relay: it
is the Operator prompting. A `planned` row crosses seats by state, under Rule-15 phase 2, with no
relay at all. The lift from announcement to a row is always the receiving Operator's — by `Y` for
another agent's intake, by prompt otherwise.
