# Falsification record — scaling the Operator's attention: a mandate for accreted rows, an exit for the board (d-work #290)

**Claim (Operator, 2026-10-14, in three parts).** (1) A standing objective, not a task: *"as the
cos session, you primary objective is to ensure that operator's attention is scaled in the way that
Operator chooses."* (2) One mutation, inside the existing Rules: batch the decision-making and carry
standing dispositions forward per d-work, *"while adhering to existing rules"*. (3) One hypothesis
about a mechanism: *"asking me to disposition a merge is indicative of a failure mode of the `d-work`
workflow."* Then, refusing revision 4 of the plan: *"batch is a solution **if** individual
dispositions can be superseded with a 'meta-disposition'."*

Rule-9 binds a preference as it binds a Rule, so this record covers **both** PRs of #290: the
preferences-zone change (the new key `accretion`, `batch-disposition-mode: always`, and the corrected
`with-done` sentence) and the agent-zone change (Rules 2, 3, 7, 15, 16, the vocabulary, the row
`mode` field, the drain and the digest play-book). The per-Rule amendments carry their own tables and
their Rule-5 pairwise statements: `dyad/falsification/rules/rule-3-d-work.md`,
`rule-2-no-self-ratify.md`, `rule-7-provenance.md`, `rule-15-d-work-phases.md`,
`rule-16-concurrent-sessions.md` and `rules-2-3-batch-disposition.md`, each under
`## Amendment — d-work #290`. The measurement every figure below rests on is
`agent-corpus/audits/2026-10-14-operator-attention.md`.

The vocabulary is **adopted, not coined** (Rule-13 property 1): `countersign:mandate`,
`countersign:implicit mode`, `countersign:explicit mode`, `countersign:basis`,
`countersign:delegate edge`, `countersign:escalate edge` and `countersign:amend`, from
`crafts/countersign/vocabulary/CRAFT.md`, whose `rules/interaction.md` §4 already writes the implicit
lifecycle and whose §7 lists three of this d-work's four pieces as owed to dyad-system and *not yet
adopted*. The Operator's "meta-disposition" is that craft's `mandate`, and nothing here renames it.

## Easy agreement (Rule-9 Conditions)

Nothing was accepted on first reading without one attack pushed to *confirmed* or *refuted*.

In revisions 1–4 the first reading was agreement with the Operator's own diagnosis — that the merge
counter-prompt was a large recurring tax — and **F1 is the attack that refuted it**: the merge
question is 3 of every disposition this ledger holds (505 at the plan's snapshot, 509 at this

In revision 5 the first reading was agreement with the Operator's own *design*, which is the harder
case, and seven attacks were pushed against it to **confirmed**: F14 (carried authority is
self-ratification), F15 (the plan file is unfenced after its `Y`), F16 (Rule-2's Binding makes a
mandate over unnamed rows a blank cheque), F17 (the Done-`Y` becomes a rubber stamp), F19 (a ratified

free inflow with no drain makes the board worse). Three of them changed the mutation rather than
being argued past: **F19** forced the attack on survivor 3–15 and its amendment, **F20** put the

## Survivors implemented

1. **The drain.** `backlog → archived` in `dyadlib.TRANSITIONS` and in Rule-16's Store sentence, in
   one commit, plus Rule-3's retirement form `Y/N: archive #a, #b, … #k?`, executed by the verb the
   store already has (`dyad dwork state <id> archived`).
   From F21.
2. **Implicit mode under a mandate.** A row whose existence the authorized plan describes, inside the
   **mandate scope** that plan states, is born `open` carrying `<date> Y plan (mandate #<id>)`, its
   record's entry 1 the mandate's own Operator words with the note `mandate #<id>, <date>`. Its Done
   rides the mandate's Done-`Y`, which names it. From the Operator's own design, fenced by F14's
   three fences, F16's stated ceiling and F20's escalate edge.
3. **Never accretable, whatever a plan says:** a destructive host action (Rule-8), a release
   (Rule-11), a Rule-3 **Intake** (another Operator's words, Rule-7 property 4), a preference change,
   and any mutation outside the plan. From F14 and the 28 Intakes among the 117 accreted rows.
4. **The report.** The completion evidence lists every row accreted under the mandate — id, mode,
   what it holds, what it cost — before the Done question, which names them. An omission is a breach
   of Rule-3. From F17: a single `Y` is verification only while the reply is readable in one sitting.
5. **Two of the three refactors `interaction.md` §7 owed**: the optional `mode` field on rows
   (`explicit` or `implicit`, absent reads `explicit`), and `amend` named in Rule-3's own
   `## Answer grammar`. The third — a mandate **record**, so a standing authorization is parsed data
   and not preference prose — is **not built**: the mandate is carried on the row (`mode` plus the
   `(mandate #<id>)` entry) and the preference stays prose. Named as owed. From F16: without the
   stated ceiling and the `mode` field the design is the blank cheque the attack names.
6. **The held PR.** A PR of a d-work not yet done waits for that d-work's Done-`Y`, rebased as `main`
   moves, with the rebase count in the completion evidence. From F18, and priced by F4 and by the two
   live instances recorded in the audit (PR #355, PR #371).

## What was proposed and is *not* implemented

- **No plan-`Y` is made a merge ratification** (three of revision 4's four grounds stand: a scope
  fence over #259's own `files touched` line matches 865 of 1,183 tracked paths, 123 of 187 stored
  plans carry no such line, and a fence reading the live preference file is self-ratification by a
  one-line branch edit).
- **No row is parked with no Operator act** (F-table's own correction, `interaction.md` I6).
- **No cap on the mandate scope**: the ceiling must be *stated*, and its size is the Operator's.
- **No kinded `disposed` parser** — `'Y plan' in row.disposed` stays a substring test, and an
  inherited authorization inherits its unreliability (attack 11 of Rule-3's amendment #290; candidate row 2 of
  `agent-corpus/audits/2026-10-08-disposition-chain-of-authority.md`).
- **The evidence block is not shortened**, and the accreted list makes it longer. Moving it to the PR
  body with its `evidence-sha256` in the reply is the larger reduction and is its own d-work.
