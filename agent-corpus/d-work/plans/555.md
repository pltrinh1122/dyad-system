## Intent as read
The Operator, on #553 revision 3's counter: "This should be included in the decision making criteria for the condition
to ask for my disposition. I agree that execution order (unless explicitly stated as requirement) is execution details

## Where the criterion lives
- **Rule-3 Plan** says: "Work that departs from the plan is a new plan and needs a new `Y`."
- **The DMC record `dyad/dmc/plan-departure.jsonld`** decides "Is this work within the plan its plan-Y authorized?",
  with three outcomes: `as-planned`, `mandate`, `new-plan`.
Neither says what *departs*, so a change of order was read as a departure (#553 revision 3, and the cos's own reading
on #532 revision 6).

## Survivor: a fourth outcome, `execution-detail`
**When:** the work is the plan's mutation unchanged in content, and one of the following differs:
- the order of its steps;
- how its PRs are split or stacked;
- which seat executes a step within its charter;
- the tooling route.

All three of these must hold:
1. the plan did not state that order or split as a **requirement** (a plan that needs an order says so, e.g.
   "order: required");
2. the end state is the same;
3. no intermediate state is irreversible or reaches the world (a release cut, a tag, a publish, a destructive host
   action, a preference value).

**Then:** authorized by the plan-`Y`, never asked. It is reported as a departure line in the completion evidence
(Rule-3 E8), so the Operator verifies it at the Done.

## Falsification
| # | attack | result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Order can change an outcome (#526: the bundle PR before the evidence fix could not cut) | **survives with condition 3**: when an intermediate state is outward or irreversible, order is not detail. #526's cut order fell under it, so its revision was rightly asked |
| 2 | "Same end state" is the agent's judgment | **survives, priced**: it is stated as a departure line in the completion evidence, and the Operator's Done-`Y` verifies it. A wrong judgment costs one Done `N` (amend), not an unauthorized outcome, because the content was authorized |
| 3 | A plan could avoid requirements to widen the agent's latitude | **refuted**: the latitude covers order and split only, never content. Content changes stay `new-plan` |
| 4 | It contradicts Rule-2 Binding ("a Y binds only to what its question named") | **refuted**: the question named the mutation. The order was the agent's narration of how, not part of what was asked, unless the plan marked it as a requirement |
| 5 | Retroactive: #553 revision 3 and #532 revision 6 cost the Operator questions | **conceded**: both would now be `execution-detail` (#532 revision 6 also moved work into a follow-up row, which stays `new-plan`). Nothing is re-done; the record notes them as the cases that motivated the criterion |
