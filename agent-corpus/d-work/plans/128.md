## Intent as read
The Operator asserts that all incident gaps have been addressed, and asks for the mitigations to be
walked one at a time — what each is, how it works, and which gap it mitigates. Two readings of
"addressed" are attacked: the strong one (a mitigation exists that stops or catches the class) and
the weak one (the gap is at least recorded and tracked). The inventory is compiled from the code
and the Rule text, not from the audit's prose, by three delegated readers, one per mode group, each
told to verify every claim in the tree.

**Verdict.** The claim is refuted on every reading that means "remedied", and refuted on the weaker
reading that means "recorded and tracked". Nothing has been implemented since the audit; five of
nine modes have no mechanical fence at all; two modes recurred within two days of being classified,
one of them turning `main` red; and the taxonomy itself has a tenth pattern it does not name. What
survives is narrower and worth keeping: three modes cannot be fenced on this kernel and are
correctly held as backlog rows rather than as gaps, and the highest-harm sub-mode has been quiet for
five days under the one structural change the system has made — on one day's evidence for the newest
half of it.
