## The claim, falsified (Rule-9)
**Claim:** there are no duplicates between guards and tests — what a guard checks, no test checks
again, so a trace's `suite` and `guards` buckets are disjoint by content.

| # | attack | result | survivor |

**Survivor.** Guards and tests duplicate execution in two live tests (a Rule-12 p1 observation for
the syseng craft, not this plan's to fix). A trace's `suite` and `guards` buckets are two kinds of
one call — the gate ran the suite, or skipped it — and the kind is decided by **what the gate ran**,
read from the pushed range and the gate's own skip line, never from a duration.
