**Ambiguous, with the reading taken:**
- **A1. What "tuning" can mean here.** The schema lets only a core record (`dyad/dmc/`) carry `dyad:tunables`,
  numbers kept as preferences. This criterion is chronicle's practice, not the core's, and its one number-like term,
  "its week", is a judgement, not a count. Read: **tuning is revision.** Each condition is one outcome, so tuning a
  condition is one edit to one record, in a craft-zone PR you approve. A number worth a preference can be raised later,
  and would move the record into the core.
- **A2. Significant or not, or a degree.** You said some benefit more than others. Read: **this record decides
  significant or not**, so the segment map includes or excludes a segment. How much it matters, and which to target, is
  the go-to-market's decision (#672), never this record's.

**The proposed outcomes**, in precedence (the first that fails gives the outcome):
1. `not-accountable` — the segment does not answer for work an AI agent did: at most a beneficiary, left off the map;
2. `costless` — an unchecked error costs it nothing real (money, safety, reputation, a regulator, a user's trust): left
   off the map;
3. `rare` — such an error does not recur within the segment's week: left off the map;
4. `significant` — all three hold: on the map, each condition's reading labelled hypothesis or evidence.
Default: a condition that cannot be decided from the record is labelled hypothesis and asked, never assumed.
