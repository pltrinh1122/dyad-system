**Names proposed (H):**
1. **Dyad Why**: you bring what you saw, it asks why with you. It ties to the product and says what it does.
2. **Why Desk**: a desk you bring a symptom to; plain, and not tied to the product brand.
3. **Root & Anchor**: the two ends every answer reaches, what causes it and what it protects.
4. **Countersign Clinic**: borrows the system's own model name; the risk is the medical overtone and a term only insiders know.
5. **Second Why**: the first why is the user's; the agent asks the next one.

**Definition of done (revised):** revision 1's four items, plus 5. a short design note for the knowledge base the agent
will read (its entries, their sources, the consent rule for `agent:` reports), in the canvas set. The agent service is a
row of its own, opened on your prompt once a name is picked.

## Names, revision 3 (prompt: provenance #701, entry 3)
Entry 3: 'the name needs to quickly resonate with the user, for example "AI Agent - why do you?"'. Read: the name is the
user's own question, in the voice of someone who just saw the symptom.
| id | attack | result | survivor |
|---|---|---|---|
| N1 | a name resonates by being clever | **refuted** | it resonates by being the sentence the user already said to themselves, or typed into a search box ("why does my AI …") |
| N2 | the question alone is enough | **survives, scoped** | "why do you?" names no symptom; a name that opens a sentence the canon completes ("… make things up?") carries the symptom too |
| N3 | a product name in it would resonate more | **confirmed, rejected** | another company's mark in the name invites a trademark problem; "AI" or "AI agent" stays generic |

**Names (H), the user's voice:**
1. **Why Does My AI…?**: completed by a canon symptom: "… make things up?" (S1), "… ignore my rules?" (S7), "… do what I
   said, not what I meant?" (S5). Recommended: each entry point is a symptom and a search phrase.
2. **AI, Why Do You?**: the operator's example, tightened.
3. **Why'd It Do That?**: the moment of surprise, said aloud.
4. **My AI Did What?**: the felt symptom, disbelief, as the name.
5. **Ask Why, Agent**: the user turning the question on the agent.
