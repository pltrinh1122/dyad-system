## Why the order is legal now, and was not yesterday
`bundle.check` (`dyad/guards/infra/bundle.py:144-149`) softens a row/tree version mismatch to a
warning only when **all four** hold: a semver parse of both, `row_v < live_v`, `(comp, row_v)` in
the tag set, and `(comp, live_v)` not in it. Yesterday `dyad-operator` 0.10.1 was not a tag, so the
third conjunct failed and the mismatch was a hard failure — which is why #241's authorized bump was
reverted and reported as incident row 109. Today `dyad-operator-v0.10.1` and `syseng-v0.4.0` exist,
so each bump lands on the warn path. This d-work is the first exercise of that path; nothing has
ever run it.
