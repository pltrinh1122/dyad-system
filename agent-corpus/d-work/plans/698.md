Proposed `dmc:takeover` (cabinet craft), for an act a unit has claimed (its board entry's owner, or its presence `rows`), precedence top first:
- `wait-retry` — the act is stalled by an outside service (a hosting 5xx, a refused connection, a timeout) and the unit is live → the home does not start it; the unit retries with backoff (four tries, 10 s doubling, as the session's git rule); the home re-reads at the next prompt. An act the home itself owns (a ledger push) is retried the same way and never handed off.
- `nudge` — the unit is live and the act waits on it (a relay unread past `inflight-unread-hours`, a doorbell not rung) → one doorbell, re-rung once; never taken over.
- `take-over` — the unit is gone (its session archived or stale past the presence window with no wake armed), or the operator asks, or the act is the home's reserved one (recording a `Y`, asking a question) → the home announces it first on the board (owner changed to the home, the old owner told by notice), and only then does it, in a fork or worktree when it is more than a ledger-only push (Guide §2 Availability). Never two writers on one file.
- `hand-on` — none of the above → the act stays with its unit.
Default: `hand-on`. A take-over is never silent: an act the home starts on a claimed row without the board's owner change is an incident.

The Guide §2 gains a paragraph naming the record: under an outage the home waits and retries before anything else; it takes over a claimed act only by the record's `take-over` outcome, announced first.

Definition of done (proposed): the record with its four outcomes and the outage case; Guide §2's paragraph; the INCIDENTS row for #697's unannounced take-over and the plan files drafted in the home's chat; a test that the record's outcomes and precedence load.
