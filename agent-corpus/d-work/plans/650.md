3. **`crafts/chronicle/templates/key.md`** (counsel): one line per paragraph, `¶<n> <first words> | record <citation>`
   or `¶<n> <first words> | fiction persona` / `fiction composites: <name>, …`. The grammar is counsel's (A2).
4. **`crafts/chronicle/guards/episodes.py`** (engineer), on a piece whose header says `form: keyed`:
   - the key exists beside it;
   - each paragraph of the piece has exactly one key line whose first words match;

   - a fiction line's composites are declared in the character list;
   - the prose holds no footnote, `*(fiction)*` marker or `fiction:` line;
