## Facts read at the base
- `touch(root, rows, files)` (`dyad/guards/agent/sessions.py`):
  - it is the only writer of a presence file;
  - it unions the previous `rows` and `files` with the ones it is given;
  - it never drops a row that has since gone terminal.
- `touch_from_open_rows`, the no-argument `session touch`, claims **every** open or planned row of the instance. This
  is #130 (its `refs` hold the description; its title is the malformed `--backlog`, which #565's sweep reports as I2).
- `session touch -r <row>` writes `files:` empty unless `-f` is also given. This is #311 (the audit of 2026-10-15,
  G1).
- Every ledger act lands through `scripts/land.py`'s worktree, in one commit per call or batch. `dyad inbox read`
  lands its read markers the same way.
- Under `organization: m4` the **home records the plan-`Y`s** for rows other units execute (provenance entries noted
  `relayed via`). The unit that runs a row learns of it from a relay or from the automaton's `row #N planned` notice in
  its inbox.

## Mutation (engineer, agent zone, one PR)
- **`sessions.py`.**
  - `files_of_rows(root, rows)`: the files the rows' stored plans name.
  - `claim(root, rows)`: the rows of `rows` that the board at `root` assigns to this unit (`DYAD_SESSION` in `owner`,
    or no owner named at all).
  - `touch` drops terminal and missing rows.
  - No-argument `session touch` claims the previous rows ∪ the board rows that name this unit as owner. It no longer
    claims every open row (#130).
  - `-r` without `-f` derives `files` (#311).
- **`land.py`.**
  - After a `new`, or a `state … planned` whose `-d` is a `Y plan`, has written in the worktree, and `DYAD_SESSION`
    is valid: refresh the actor's presence there, claiming the row only when `claim` gives it.
  - The same applies to every such line of a batch.
  - The presence path joins the commit.
- **`inbox.py`.** `inbox read` refreshes the reader's presence in the same land, claiming the rows of the relays and
  `planned` notices it marks read, through `claim`.
- **Tests:**
  - an owner's plan-`Y` land carries its presence file, with the row and the plan's files;
  - the home's plan-`Y` for another unit's row refreshes its `seen` and claims nothing;
  - an inbox read of a relay claims the row;
  - a terminal row leaves the file;
  - no-argument `touch` claims only board-owned rows;
  - `-r` derives `files`;
  - a land found already landed carries no presence change.
- **`dyad board resume`:** the informational `last ledger act <age>` per seat (F8).
- **`dyad/VERSION`:** working bump if released since.
- **#130 and #311:** this row delivers both rows' mutations. The completion evidence names them, and the home may
  retire them by the retirement form after the Done. No other row is opened.

## Counter and reconciliation (Rule-10)
- **Path:** each unit's own file is refreshed at the acts that hand it a row (its own plan-`Y` or new row, its inbox
  read). Rows are gated by the board's `owner`. Files come from the claimed rows' plans.
- **Strongest counter:** gating on the board couples presence to the cabinet's board. A system without a board entry
  for a row (or `organization: flat`) has no owner to read.
- **Reconciliation:** with no owner named, the actor claims the row. That is the flat case, where the actor is the
  executor. Under m4 every relayed row has a board entry, because the relay guard already refuses a relay that names
  no owner (#536). So the gate never leaves a row unclaimed by everyone, and it never claims for the home a row it
  only recorded.
