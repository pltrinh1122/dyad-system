**Ambiguous, with the reading taken:**
- **A1. Order: problems first or targets first.** Reviewing every node of every segment costs the most and decides
  least. Read: **top-down**. Agree the problem classes (four lines), then pick targets on the map, then review in depth
  only the targets' roots and anchors. Untargeted segments stay hypotheses, untouched.
- **A2. What a phase's answer is.** These are review steps, not Rule-3 questions. Each phase ends in one question in
  plain form ("accept, or amend?"). Your answer is entered verbatim as a `prompt` entry of this row, so nothing
  ratifies mid-way. The one ratification is the done-`Y` on the PR that records the alignment.
- **A3. Where the alignment lives.** `site/lean-canvas/index.md` gains a **Targets** section: the targets in your
  words, and per target its classes, roots and anchor branch by node id. Amended node wording goes into the segment's
  source tree as a superseding node (§6 append-only), and the projection is rebuilt. One infra-zone PR.
