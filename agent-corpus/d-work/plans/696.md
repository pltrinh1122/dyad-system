## Plan
**The play-book.** `crafts/chronicle/playbooks/alignment-review.md`. Crafts keep play-books beside their rules, as
`crafts/channels/playbooks/` and `crafts/cabinet/playbooks/` do. Its sections:
- **Purpose:** the operator and the agent align on target segments and each target's problems: classes, roots, anchor branch.
- **Inputs:** a canvas set judged by §5, its projection and source trees.
- **Steps:** the four phases of the reading, each with its view, its one question in plain form and how its answer is
  entered (a `prompt` entry; nothing ratifies before the done-`Y`).
- **Knobs:** the named parameters a run may set, each with its default, so that tuning means changing a default:
  - **order**: top-down, or bottom-up;
  - **targets**: at most 3;
  - **batch**: whether phases 1 and 2 share a reply; default no;
  - **depth**: the targets only, or every segment;
  - **tree-first**: a target with no source tree gets one, derived independently, before phase 3; default always.
- **Run record:** what every run leaves in the canvas set, `<canvas set>/reviews/<row>.md`:
  - the knobs as set;
  - each phase's question and your answer, by provenance entry;
  - what was amended;
  - the time per phase, from the row's trace;
  - the agent's proposed tunings, each **H**.
- **Tuning:** a tuning is a craft PR on your prompt that changes a step or a knob's default, citing the run records
  behind it. A play-book changes only that way (Rule-13: recurrence proposes, the operator disposes).
- **Evidence:** the run record and the canvas set's Targets section.

`collateral.md` §5 gains one line pointing to it. The craft guard and the tests see a new Markdown file only.

**This run** is the play-book's first, with knobs at their defaults:
1. **Phase 1:** a review page of the four problem classes, each with its roots and the segments that suffer it. Your
   answer is entered as a `prompt`.
2. **Phase 2:** a review page of segment cards in rank order, each with its phase-1 classes. You pick 1–3 targets and
   settle the tie or the axes where they touch the targets.
3. **Phase 3:** for each target with no source tree, one is derived first by a fresh drafter briefed without any other
   tree, as #685 did, and projected. Then one review page per target, slicing its source tree to its roots, the
   branches that reach them, the anchor branch and its symptoms in the segment's words. You accept, or amend a node,
   and an amended node is written as a superseding node.
4. **Phase 4:** the PRs, then the Done question.

**Mandate scope:** none. The drafters are subagents, not rows, and every tree they derive lands in this row's infra PR.

**The record, as drafted.**
