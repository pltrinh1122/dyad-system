**Survivor (the criterion this row would record as a DMC, `judgement-complexity`).** A recurring judgement in a
play-book step is **similarity-derivable**, not complex, when all of these hold:
1. its outcomes are a finite label set;
2. a fixed, versioned comparator set of past judgements labelled with those outcomes exists, with a digest;
3. a fixed vectorizer (stdlib TF-IDF over the set's frozen vocabulary) and a fixed threshold and margin (tunables)
   are used;
4. on a held-out sample of recorded judgements, agreement meets a stated bar;
5. its result is a proposal or pre-class, never a disposition, and never the trigger of a destructive act;
6. a score inside the margin, or a fall in measured agreement, returns the case to inference.

Otherwise it is **complex** (inference), unless #641's test makes it **mechanical**.
