**Survivor (the criterion this row would record as a DMC, `judgement-complexity`).** A recurring judgement in a
play-book step is **similarity-derivable**, not complex, when all of these hold:
1. its outcomes are a finite label set;
2. a fixed, versioned comparator set of past judgements labelled with those outcomes exists, with a digest;
3. a fixed vectorizer (stdlib TF-IDF over the set's frozen vocabulary) and a fixed threshold and margin (tunables)
   are used;
4. on a held-out sample of recorded judgements, agreement meets a stated bar;
5. its result is a proposal or pre-class, never a disposition, and never the trigger of a destructive act;
6. a score inside the margin, or a fall in measured agreement, returns the case to inference.

Otherwise it is **complex** (inference), unless #641's test makes it **mechanical**.

## Revision 2 (2026-11-08): the similarity class is precedent
Your prompt (provenance #642, entry 4) is "fold precedent into #642". It follows the home's read-only falsification
of "precedent is a type of judgement similarity can derive" (Q1–Q7, whose survivor is below). The revision sharpens
the class the plan builds. It adds no PR and no unit.

**Changes to the Mutation:**
1. **The kind is named `precedent`**, not `similarity`, and `KINDS` gains `precedent`. A precedent record carries
   `dyad:precedents` in place of `dyad:comparators`. The guard, schema and `dmc.md` say so. The rest of step 1's
   guard rules stand: the path, its sha256, labels equal to the outcome names, agreement at or above the bar, and no
   disposition or destructive outcome.
2. **The precedent set holds ratified cases only (Q4).** Each entry has:
   - the case's decisive inputs, the record's `dyad:inputs` as stored facts, not the whole text (Q2);
   - the outcome it set (Q1);
   - the row whose done-`Y` or plan-`Y` covered it.

   An agent ruling no `Y` covered is not a precedent. `similar.py`'s `freeze` refuses an entry with no covering row
   on `main`.
3. **Superseded cases drop out (Q5).** The set is rebuilt when a Rule cited in the record's `dyad:owner` or `home`
   is amended, or when a later done-`Y` names a case's row under `retires` or supersedes it. `freeze` records the
   base commit, and the guard warns when the owner Rule moved after it.
4. **Distinguishing stays inference (Q3, Q6).** `label` returns `abstain` when below the threshold, inside the
   margin, or when there is no precedent at all (first of its kind). The play-book's class step then sends the case
   to the agent, which may also depart from a matched precedent by naming the input that differs. That departure is
   recorded in the reply, and becomes a precedent only when a later `Y` covers it.
5. **Agent-side calls only (Q7).** `judgement-complexity`'s similarity-derivable outcome is renamed
   **precedent-derivable**, and its conditions are the survivor's six. A record whose outcome is a disposition, or
   whose consequence stands in for an operator's `Y`, is never `precedent`.
6. **The first run's candidate list** names the agent-side records with ratified cases on file. The obvious one is
   `plan-departure` (execution detail against departure), with today's rulings on #626, #640 and #641 and their
   covering Dones as the set. Each remains a candidate row, not a change.

**Survivor (Q1–Q7), as the record's precedent-derivable conditions:**
1. the comparison set is ratified precedents, each labelled with the outcome it set;
2. similarity is over the record's decisive inputs;
3. the set is frozen per version, and rebuilt at a Rule change or a superseding Done;
4. the threshold and margin are fixed, and below or inside them the case goes to the agent;
5. a case that may differ from its precedent stays with the agent;
6. the result settles only agent-side calls, never a disposition.
