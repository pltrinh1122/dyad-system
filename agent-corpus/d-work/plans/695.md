## Intent reading (revision 1)

Prompt (entry 1): "i think it's appropriate for us to implement a "Recommended for next:" with that are the outcomes of triggers.  ideally these are mechanically generated; however, i believe it'll require judgement-inference on your part."

| portion | class | against |
|---|---|---|
| a "Recommended for next:" part | new, adjacent | the closing line (Rule-3 Batch disposition, `dyad board`) has two parts: Awaiting you, the questions asked and not answered, and Coming, the pending queue (rows ready for a question the agent has not yet asked). Neither shows what a trigger says is due and nobody has started: an audit due, a grooming due, a hardening row due, a backlog row promotion would propose, a question owed again |
| the outcomes of triggers | overlap | the triggers exist or are building: `dmc:groom-trigger`, `dmc:promotion`, `dmc:audit-trigger` (#691), `dmc:hardening-due` (#692), `dmc:re-present` (#694); `dyad board resume` already prints some of their inputs as stall lines |
| ideally mechanical; judgement needed | holds, and it shapes the design | some outcomes are mechanical (audit due A1–A3 from the sweep, groom G2 and G4, re-present `remind` and `pending`, hardening W1, the ceiling); others are the home's inference (hardening H1, promotion's adjacency and impact, re-present `full`). If the home wrote the line by hand it would repeat the incidents of 2026-11-10 ("Coming: 2 plan" written from expectation). So the inferred outcomes are recorded first, and the line is generated from records alone |

Proposed:
- The closing line gains a third part, after Coming: `Recommended for next: <n> <kind> (<ids or names>), …`, each kind the trigger it comes from: `audit`, `groom`, `hardening #id`, `promote #id`, `re-present #id`. Shown only when non-zero, under a preference `recommended-next` (`on` | `off`, the template ships `on`), as `coming` governs its part.
- `dyad board` generates it from two sources: the mechanical outcomes it computes itself (the newest sweep report's `audit due` line, the groom trigger's G2/G4, the re-present outcome of each owed question), and a board key `recommended:` the home writes by `dyad board set _cos` for each inferred outcome, one entry per item with its trigger, its outcome code and a one-line reason (write-ahead: recorded before the reply that shows it). The line never comes from the reply's text.
- A record, `dmc:recommended-next`, decides what enters: an item a trigger record says is due, not yet asked or started, and not already counted in Awaiting you or Coming; at most the promotion ceiling (2) of `promote` items; an item that a standing mandate starts unasked (hardening under `hardening-mandate: on`) reads `starting`, not recommended; an inferred entry older than its trigger's input (the board moved since it was written) is dropped, with a warning, until re-recorded.
- It authorizes nothing (a form preference, as `coming`): a recommendation becomes work only by the question its own trigger asks, or by the operator's prompt.

Definition of done (proposed): the third part generated by `dyad board` from the mechanical outcomes and the `recommended:` key; the record; the preference row in the template; the Rule-3 sentence and the `awaiting you` vocabulary row naming the part, with the amendment; a test of each kind, of the cap, of a stale inferred entry dropped and of `off`. Built by engineer-1537d8fb and counsel-0032b4c0, one agent-zone PR, after #691, #692 and #694 land, since it reads their records.
