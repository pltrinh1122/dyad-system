## What a subagent may do (Rule-3 Scope, E7 #107; `delegation: plan-and-execute`)

counter-prompt, pushes, opens or updates a PR, merges, or runs a host action. A relay to a seat (R29) and a
question to the Operator are the leader's own acts: they stay with the main Agent.
