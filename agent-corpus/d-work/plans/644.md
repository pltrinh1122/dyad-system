## Intent as read: the intent reading (`intent-reading: on`, Rule-3 Plan)
Prompt: provenance #644, entry 1. It follows the home's read-only answers:

- case records exist only as scattered prose, and the completion evidence that names a ruling is not stored.

**The approach proposed (cheapest first).** Each step uses the cheapest decider that can do it. You answer one
question per batch, and the Done is that question.

1. **Admission becomes mechanical.** A new record, `dyad/dmc/precedent-admission.jsonld` (kind mechanical, home
   `scripts/similar.py`), is decided in `freeze`. A case is **admitted** only when all of these hold:
   - its ruling is named in a stored line of the covering row, as below;
   - no later Done, Rule amendment or overruling record supersedes it;
   - its outcome was not given by a precedent record;
   - its record is agent-side.

   Otherwise `freeze` refuses it, naming the reason: `uncovered`, `unnamed`, `superseded`, `self-derived` or
   `operator-side`.
2. **The decision log: new cases are named by construction.** `<instance>/d-work/decisions/<row>.jsonl` holds one
   line per ruling the completion evidence names: the record, a case id, the decisive inputs (structured fields and
   prose), the outcome, and the row. It is written by `dwork state … --decisions <file>` in the same clerical commit
   that records the `Y`, so your `Y` covers exactly the named lines, and nothing waits on reconstruction again. This
   is also revision 3's missing audit-sample store.
3. **Backfill, by the cheapest decider each source allows:**
   - **(a) Structured sources, by code.** The change log's class and actor cells; `Y archive (…)`, `Y open (promotion
     …)` and `Y release` entries; and verdict tables whose result column holds the record's own outcome name. These
     are parsed into decision lines. No inference.
   - **(b) Prose sources, by two readers.** Plan revisions, departure notes, inbox rulings and falsification tables
     are labelled blind by Haiku and the default model. A case is kept only when both agree with each other and with
     the source's own wording. Every disagreement is set aside on a list: the context-gap list, never resolved by the
     agent alone.
   - **(c) Admission is your `precedent-Y`, not the Done** (provenance #644, entry 2). The backfilled lines are
     listed by record, with counts and the set-aside list, before one question, `Y/N: admit as precedents <n> cases
     of <records>?`. Its `Y` admits exactly the listed lines.
4. **The context gaps the blind test found are fixed in the record text, so the backfill reads clean:**
   - `attack-result`: outcome names that read one way only (home Rule-9);
   - `release-class`: where a decision record plus its test sits, settled against #621's precedent (home
     `release.md`);
   - `retirement-candidate`: cases older than its grounds are excluded (home `disposition-digest.md`);
   - `craft-instantiation`: each criterion's firing condition stated, for Haiku's sake (home
     `craft-instantiation.md`).
5. **The `precedent-Y`: a fourth ratification event** (your prompt, entry 2). A ruling becomes a precedent only by
   your `Y` to a question naming it as one. The Done's coverage, which ratifies the work and not each ruling inside
   it, no longer suffices: it is the weakness found in the home's answer on valid precedents.
   - Rule-3 gains the question, its batch form, and a `Y precedent (…)` disposition entry, as a ratification event.
     Rule-2 applies by reference.
   - The vocabulary gains `precedent-Y`.
   - The decision log marks a line `candidate` when the Done records it, and `precedent` when a `precedent-Y` names it.
   - `precedent-admission` admits only `precedent` lines.
   - **Cadence, to keep your attention low:** candidates wait, and one `precedent-Y` is asked per batch, at the next
     grooming run or once at least 5 are pending, whichever is first, and never inside a Done question (one kind per
     question, Rule-3 Batch).
6. **First conversions.** A record whose admitted set reaches the bar (proposed: at least 5 cases, with agreement on a
   held-out share of at least 0.8) is converted to kind `precedent`, with its frozen set. Below the bar, it stays
   inference, and the report says how many cases it lacks.
