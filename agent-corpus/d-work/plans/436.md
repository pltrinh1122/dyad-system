# Plan #436 — `delegation-posture`: the cos's efficiency-vs-responsiveness setting

## Intent as read
The Operator wants to select, as a preference, how much the cos seat delegates. Three settings:
`responsive` (the cos delegates everything it can, so it is always free for the Operator's next matter),
`balanced`, `efficient` (the cos does more itself; fewer orchestration messages, fewer tokens, longer stretches
when the cos is busy). The trade-off is explicit: every delegated act costs a hop (a message out, a message
back, a re-read of the result) in time and tokens; every act the cos keeps costs the Operator the cos's
attention for its duration.

## Evidence from this seat (2026-10-24/25)
- 76 commits on `main` since midnight, 43 of them this seat's ledger-only commits; each costs seconds. The acts
  that cost minutes were the ones #432 moved out: two evidence runs of 10–20 min (one invalid for sharing the
  checkout), one preferences PR pushed through the full gate (~8 min of hook), five falsification analyses and
  every plan file written at the cos.
- The #412–#433 cycle (22 rows) took about 45 relay messages between the cos and three secretaries, roughly
  two per row, each a few hundred tokens — small beside one fork: plan #425's tracking fork cost ~350k tokens
  over 50 minutes to verify heads the secretaries had already verified.
- So the overhead the Operator names is real and asymmetric: a **secretary** hop is cheap (hundreds of tokens,
  the secretary's context already loaded); a **fork** is dear (it re-reads the cos's whole context) and worth it
  only for work longer than a few minutes or that must not block the cos.

## Survivor
A form preference `delegation-posture` — `responsive` \| `balanced` \| `efficient` — read by the cabinet Guide's cos
section (#432 C1) under `delegation: plan-and-execute`; inert under `delegation: none`. The cos's five acts
(receive, route, present, relay, board) are the floor under every value. The values move everything above it:

| act above the floor | `responsive` | `balanced` | `efficient` |
|---|---|---|---|
| plan file for a prompt-born row | fork or owning secretary | the cos if < 2 min of tool time, else out | the cos |
| falsification / analysis | counsel (charter) | counsel; the cos if one-table and < 2 min | the cos, counsel for Rule text |
| evidence verification before a Done question | secretary's block quoted; fork re-runs in a worktree | secretary's block quoted; no re-run | secretary's block quoted; no re-run |
| board rendering, head checks | fork, continuous | the cos at each check-in | the cos at each check-in |
| branch, push, PR, merge of any zone | owning secretary | owning secretary | owning secretary (Rule-3 Scope: never a subagent) |
| cos time per Operator turn | seconds | ≤ 2 min | minutes to tens of minutes |
| messages and tokens | most (≈2 hops per act + a fork's context) | middle | fewest |

`balanced`'s budget is the one number the Operator tunes: 2 minutes of tool time at the cos, the length of a
ledger-only push plus a short read. Route order under every value: a secretary whose charter covers the act first;
a fork only for work no charter covers or that must not block the cos.
dyad-system: `responsive` — the Operator's words ("highly available to attend to operator and their new matters",
#432) — with the token cost stated: a fork per long act, and a counsel or engineer round-trip for every draft.
