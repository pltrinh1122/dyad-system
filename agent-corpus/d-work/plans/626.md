## Mutation
1. **C0 first** (`order: required`: every other child's guard check depends on it).
   - **Master rule.** A record whose `dyad:home` is a play-book or run-book (`playbooks/` or `runbooks/`) is the
     master. Its outcomes, conditions, consequences and precedence are defined there alone.
   - **Citation.** The home cites it once, as `dmc:<identifier>` (the record's `identifier`), at the step that
     applies it.
   - **Records homed in an Agent Rule** (`dyad/rules/`) or a craft rule are unchanged. The Rule stays the master, and
     the outcome-name check stays as it is (A1).
   - **Guard** (`dmc.py`), for master records:
     - fail when the home does not cite `dmc:<identifier>`;
     - fail when the home names every outcome of the record, which means restated;
     - one-to-one with the citations: a `dmc:<id>` that names no record fails.
   - **Text.** `dmc.md` step 3 and the schema's "outcome names are quoted from the home file" are revised to say
     the same. The rule of one definition per decision holds.
2. **C1–C12, each the same procedure on its files:**
   - **(a)** Every decision the prose states becomes a record: an existing one revised to master, or a new one in
     `dyad/dmc/` (in `crafts/<craft>/dmc/` for a craft's). It must pass `dmc.py`, and its home stays the play-book.
   - **(b)** Every mechanical step becomes a command:
     - an existing `dyad` verb or run-book `dyad-cmd` step, or a new one, with its test;
     - read-only, the automaton sweep's part on its trigger (`check-trigger` classes the new part);
     - reversible, the automaton's only from a recorded `Y` (Rule-8);
     - destructive, a question. Each stays where its class puts it.
   - **(c)** The prose keeps only Trigger, Steps, Question and Evidence. A step reads "`dmc:<id>`", "`dyad <verb>`" or
     "ask `<form>`". Rationale and history leave for git and the provenance the play-book already cites.
     Each child's tests are adjusted to the cited form (`dyad/tests/test_playbooks.py`, `test_runbook.py`,
     `test_steps.py`, and each new verb's test).
   - **(d)** Measured: words before and after, of the file and of a run. A run's words are the play-book, its
     run-book and the records the run's steps cite.
3. **Order after C0** is execution detail. The children run in parallel by unit, each PR based on C0's head:
   - counsel and engineer work C1–C9 in pairs, the largest first (C1, C2, C3);
   - distribute works C10–C11;
   - operate works C12.
4. **Verification and the Done.** The home runs `dyad check --evidence` on every PR head. The Done names #626, the
   13 children, and each PR with its head, plus the before/after table.
