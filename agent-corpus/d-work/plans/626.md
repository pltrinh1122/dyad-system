## Definition of done (proposed, for the intent-Y to accept)
1. **The DMC record is the master.** `dmc.md`, `dyad/dmc/schema.md` and the `dmc` guard: a play-book-homed decision
   is defined in its record. The play-book cites it by id, and the guard fails a play-book that restates outcomes.
2. **Every play-book and run-book refactored** (26 files). Each step is one of three things: a DMC id, an automaton or
   runner command, or a question with its form. Every decision rule the prose held is in a DMC record, and every
   mechanical step is a command the automaton or the runner executes, within Rule-8's classes.
3. **Measured.** A before/after table of words per file and per run, in the completion evidence.
4. **Nothing changes in behaviour.** Every existing test and guard passes, with the play-book tests adjusted to
   the cited form. Any step whose behaviour would change is named and asked.
