The proposal adds no limit on what the home may present. It limits how many kinds wait on the operator at once: a reply still asks one question (Rule-10), and the closing line still counts every question outstanding.

Ambiguous, with leans:
- **(h) the bound's unit.** It could be outstanding kinds or outstanding questions. Lean: kinds, since the switching cost is between modes, and a batch of five plans is one mode.
- **(i) whether every Done is solo by default.** Lean: no. A Done is solo when it ratifies merges, a release or retirements past the threshold, or is the first of its record; otherwise Dones of one tier batch, as today.

Definition of done (proposed):

- `dyad board` computes, for every question ready to ask, its outcome and the next question to ask, and the closing line's Coming shows `deferred <n>`.
- Rule-3 Batch disposition's clause for the solo outcome, the play-book's step for presenting, and the template preference rows.
- Tests:
  - destructive and release questions are always solo, whatever a setting says;
  - two plan questions with no solo input batch;
  - a Done ratifying merges past the threshold is solo;
  - a third kind waits while two are outstanding;
  - an operator's setting changes the outcome with no code change;
  - the order within a tier is #705's.

**Rules filter, judgement orders** (entry 5, the home's falsification C1–C6, carried here). The next-best is computed
in two stages:
1. **Eligibility, by rule.** A question is eligible only when every predecessor on a **gating** edge is answered. The
   edges are already in the records, though nothing yet computes question eligibility from them:
   - a row's `refs` it depends on, and `parent #N`;
   - `blocked` naming another row;
   - a plan's `order: required` with its `step:` lines;
   - the parent-Done gate (Rule-3 Ledger: a parent's Done is askable only when every child is terminal, blocked,
     backlog or done-recorded);
   - an answer that consumes another's: one question's plan or reading cites another row's still-unanswered question.
   Edges are **typed**. "Merges after" (a PR cut after another's lands) orders landing and never gates asking, so it does
   not filter.
2. **Order, by judgement**, among the eligible only: (k)'s order of #705's attention order, criticality and age, weighted
   per operator. Judgement never overrides an edge.

A dependency judgement suspects but no record holds is **flagged**: the home records it as an edge, or the operator
decides it. It is never acted on silently.

**Fine-tuning is measured, and tuned only on the operator's answer.**
- For each answer the board records, the home logs whether the operator answered the question presented as next-best or another first: the uptake of next-best, by kind and criticality tier. It is a reference count on the board's own records, holding no question text, as #705's uptake is.
- At the weekly review (riding grooming, as #705's goal review does), the home reads the count. Where the operator keeps answering something other than the next-best, it proposes one weight change with that evidence.
- The change is applied only on the operator's `Y`. A weight is the operator's setting, and the agent never writes one on its own, as Rule-2 says of a preference value.
