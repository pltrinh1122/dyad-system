**Rules filter, judgement orders** (entry 5, the home's falsification C1–C6, carried here). The next-best is computed
in two stages:
1. **Eligibility, by rule.** A question is eligible only when every predecessor on a **gating** edge is answered. The
   edges are already in the records, though nothing yet computes question eligibility from them:
   - a row's `refs` it depends on, and `parent #N`;
   - `blocked` naming another row;
   - a plan's `order: required` with its `step:` lines;
   - the parent-Done gate (Rule-3 Ledger: a parent's Done is askable only when every child is terminal, blocked,
     backlog or done-recorded);
   - an answer that consumes another's: one question's plan or reading cites another row's still-unanswered question.
   Edges are **typed**. "Merges after" (a PR cut after another's lands) orders landing and never gates asking, so it does
   not filter.
2. **Order, by judgement**, among the eligible only: (k)'s order of #705's attention order, criticality and age, weighted
   per operator. Judgement never overrides an edge.

A dependency judgement suspects but no record holds is **flagged**: the home records it as an edge, or the operator
decides it. It is never acted on silently.

### PR 1 — the page, the filter and the outcomes (agent zone; craft PR for the Guide)

**Board keys** (on an entry, written write-ahead with its `question` by `dyad board set`, or by `board advance … asked`):
- `what`, `why`, `how`: one line each, the card's WHAT/WHY/HOW, composed by the asker once, when it asks.
- `counter`, `reconcile`: one line each, Rule-10's counter and reconciliation.
- `present`: `solo` with a one-line reason, set by the home for a criticality input code cannot see (novelty, coupling,
  money or credentials). Absent means the computed outcome.
- `gates`: a space-separated list of `#<id>[:<kind>]`, the gating predecessors judgement recorded (C6). A suspected
  edge is recorded here by the home, or put to the operator, and never inferred by code.

All six are optional, so no entry stored before them migrates.

**Eligibility** (`board.eligible`), from records alone. A question is held back while any gating predecessor is
unanswered. The gating edges:
- the entry's `gates:` list;
- the parent-Done gate (Rule-3 Ledger: a parent's Done is not asked while a child is not terminal, blocked, backlog or
  done-recorded);
- a plan's `order: required` `step:` lines naming another row's question.

A row's plain `refs` is untyped and never gates alone, and "merges after" never gates (C5). A held-back question shows
as `gated <n>` in Coming, with its predecessor named on its card.

**Outcomes** (`board.presentation`), over the eligible questions.
- **Solo:**
  - always for kind `destructive` and `release` (the core floor, Rule-8 and Rule-11);
  - for a `done` whose binding size reaches `solo-binding-min` (default 3: PRs it merges plus rows it accretes or
    retires);
  - and for any entry the home marked `present: solo`.
- **Batch:** with others of the same kind otherwise, in Rule-3's batch form.
- **Defer:** a ready question whose kind would make more than `open-kinds-max` kinds outstanding (default 2; a solo
  outstanding counts as the bound), or any question while a solo is outstanding. It is recorded, never refused, and
  shown as `deferred <n>` in Coming.

Until PR 2 the order inside an outcome is the order asked, oldest first. `re-present` stays `dmc:re-present`'s.

**Cards** (`dyad board cards [--full] [--html <file>]`, read-only):
- One card per outstanding or ready question, in the presentation's order: solo first, then batches, then deferred and
  gated. Each card holds:
  - the exact `Y/N` line;

  - the counter and the reconciliation;

- Headed by the board commit and its time.
- Other systems' questions from the attention ledger (#700) are listed by reference only.
- `--html` writes one self-contained page to a path outside every repository (the home's scratch directory). Nothing
  tracked holds it.
- A card for a field the entry lacks prints `(see plan)` with the path, never invented text.
