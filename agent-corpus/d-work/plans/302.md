## Survivor (the stack, restated)
A seat is as #295 says, and its record now holds seat, holder and host. Traffic is #253's three
planes. The cos touches two: it **announces** (undirected; today by state on `main`, and, in the
store's absence, by directed notice that obliges nothing) and it **relays** — publishes an Operator's
verbatim prompt as an announcement whose content is the Operator's, which opens a row within one
system and is an intake across systems. The command plane (dispositions) has no carrier and the cos
is never on it. Hand-carry is not relay: it is the Operator prompting. The lift from announcement to
a row is always the receiving Operator's — by `Y` for another agent's intake, by prompt otherwise.
