## Intent as read
With the plan-`Y` of #558 the Operator wrote: "ensure that the decision-making criteria is codified as presented." The
criteria are the answer of 2026-11-02 to "what is the decision making criteria for expediting/accelerating/
turbo-charging outstanding work? add more 'solo' vs. each 'solo' add more 'subagents'?". They come in two steps:
- **Step 0, what holds the work up:**
  - a disposition owed by the Operator,
  - dependency order,
  - merge races on `main`,
  - or work inside a unit.

  Concurrency helps only the last.
- **Step 1, solo or subagent,** on seven criteria:
  - charter and files,
  - the acts needed (Rule-3 keeps rows, pushes, PRs and merges out of a subagent),
  - lifetime,
  - where the solo is stuck,
  - cost at the home,
  - context,
  - isolation.

  Two tie-breaks apply: an idle solo before a new one; one writer per file.
