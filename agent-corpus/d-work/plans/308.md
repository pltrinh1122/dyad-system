## Intent as read
The Operator's prompt was given in countersign-system and is relayed here verbatim (provenance entry
1, `relayed via countersign-system-3`). It asks for a self-diagnostic that countersign-system runs to
verify its own integrity, one level at a time: L1 unit-test code, L2 integration-test code, L3
unit-invariant inference, L4 system-invariant inference.

The authorized plan is countersign-system's `agent-corpus/d-work/plans/3.md` (revisions 1-4). Its
plan-`Y` was given on 2026-10-15 in the batch #3,#4 of that ledger. This row is the authoring half
the plan names: the craft is authored here, in the craft zone, and countersign-system installs it.
The model is revision 2's 2x2 grid:
- unit × coded check is L1;
- system × coded check is L2;
- unit × prose claim, with its attack, is L3;
- system × prose claim, with its attack, is L4.

Each level gates the next. Every step is read-only. A run writes one record.
