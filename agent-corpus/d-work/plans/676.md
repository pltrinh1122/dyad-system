**What exists.**

  or returned to the sender.
- **Nothing decides what happens after admission** when the sender is a downstream system of the same operator. That
  is: which part upstream builds, which part stays downstream, and what is said back down.
- **The craft/instance line** (Rule-11 property 1) and `dmc:bundled-craft`'s purpose ground say what a craft may hold
  (no instance state, and purpose judged on the craft's own tree). They are never read as one decision for a hand-up.

**Ambiguous, with the reading taken:**
- **A1. Granularity.** Read: per requirement and per constraint of the hand-up, so one hand-up can split. The parts

  settle them later.

## Mutation
1. **`dyad/dmc/intake-split.jsonld`** (new, core, an index record homed in Rule-3):
   - **`dyad:decides`:** for each requirement and constraint of an admitted hand-up from a downstream system of the same
     operator, where its work lives.
   - **`dyad:inputs`:**
     - the requirement's own words and the operator's words it cites, never the sender's code;
     - whether every installing operator needs it, or one instance's purpose or domain alone;
     - whether it needs instance data: another party's records, credentials, one client's domain, or a location;
     - which Rule, core record or craft already owns the concern;
     - whether its operator has settled it, or it rests on one of the hand-up's open questions.
   - **Outcomes, each with its `dyad:effect` (A3):**
     - `upstream-core`, **upstream core** (`shapes`): every installing operator needs it, it needs no instance data,
       and the concern is a core Rule's or a core record's. Built here, in `dyad/`, by a row of its own.
     - `upstream-craft`, **upstream craft** (`shapes`): every installing operator of one practice needs it, and it
       needs no instance data. Built here, in a Tended craft, existing or new by the craft play-book.
     - `downstream-instance`, **downstream instance** (`shapes`): only the sender's instance needs it, or it is
       instance data. Stays downstream.
     - `both`, **both** (`shapes`): a mechanism every installing operator needs, with a configuration, schema or data
       only the sender's instance holds. Upstream builds the mechanism, and downstream keeps the configuration.
     - `asked-back`, **asked back** (`asks`): its operator has not settled it. It goes back down as a question, and
       nothing is built until it is answered.
   - **The privacy rule (A2), stated in `dyad:precedence`:** another party's records, and every credential, are always
     downstream data. A requirement that needs them can put a mechanism upstream (`both`), never the data. Then
     `asked-back` before any other outcome, `both` before either single side, and core before craft when both fit.
   - **`dyad:default`:** `asked-back`, since a requirement the inputs cannot place is a question for its operator.
   - **The rest:** `dyad:judgedBy` inference, with no implementation. `dyad:home` is
     `rules/RULE-3-d-work.md`, owner `Rule-3`, and `dyad:alsoIn` holds `dmc/intake-shape.jsonld` and
     `dmc/bundled-craft.jsonld`.
   - **The hand-down:** the record's consequence for the whole hand-up is one line per item, its outcome and its ground.
     The home drafts it and shows it to the operator, and it is sent only on the operator's `Y`.
