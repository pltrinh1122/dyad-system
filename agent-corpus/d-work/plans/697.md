## Intent reading (revision 3)

Prompt (entry 3): "update playbook for presenting "undecided" for my disposition as part of grooming." This replaces revision 2's G2 (an `undecided` row asked only after two readings): an `undecided` row is presented for the operator's disposition in the run that reads it.

How, within the play-book's one question per run (`dyad/playbooks/backlog-groom.md`, Question):
- The run's one question stays the retirement form, `Y/N: archive #a, #b, … #k?`. Its list holds the rows with a retirement ground (`overtaken`, `premise-gone`), then the `unwanted-candidate` rows (G1), then the `undecided` rows, each group headed, and each row with what it holds, its ground or, for an `undecided` row, what the reading could not settle and the home's lean (keep or archive) with its reason.
- A `Y` archives every listed row; a bare `N` archives none and every row stays; an `N` with text naming rows to keep is an amend (Rule-3 Answer grammar) and the form is asked again without them, in the same run. A row kept by the operator's answer is recorded as read `holds`, its ground the operator's keep, so it is not presented again before two readings.
- A run with no row in any group asks nothing, as today.
- The rows presented are bounded by the run's reading (five, or a tenth of the backlog, G3), so the form stays short.

Definition of done gains: the play-book's Question and Evidence sections naming the three groups and the amend route; the record's `undecided` outcome naming "presented in that run's retirement form, with what is missing and the home's lean"; a test that an `undecided` row reaches the form in its first run.
