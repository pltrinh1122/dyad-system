Base commit of main: 3c51c9d

## Intent as read
Rule-3's strictness fitted the earlier regime: one pair, no automaton, no mandate, every act its own question. The
system has since grown units, a home, carried authority, an automaton and definitions of done. The operator asks for
Rule-3 to be **refactored**, not amended again, and for the new text to supersede the previous rules.

## Definition of done (proposed; amend with N and text)
A **clause map** you can read in one pass: every sentence of today's Rule-3 with its fate (kept, merged, moved to the
Rule that owns its concern, or dropped), the reason, and the row or incident it came from. Beside it, the new Rule-3,
with its length against today's.

## Revision 2 (2026-11-04): the operator's definition of done
The operator's words (provenance #585, the latest prompt entry):

> Definition of done for Rule-3 refactoring:
> - claims must passes falsification
> - claim should be re-scoped to avoid exceptions

They replace the proposed definition (the clause map). The clause map stays, as the record of what moved where.

**What it asks of the new Rule-3 (PR #670 at 9bd2481a).**
1. **Every claim is falsified.** A claim is a sentence that asserts what the Rule requires, permits or forbids; a
   pointer or a definition cited from another Rule is not one. Each claim gets at least one attack in the Rule-3
   falsification record (amendment #585), with its result and its survivor (Rule-9). Only survivors stay in the Rule.
2. **No claim carries an exception.** Every "except", "unless", "with one exemption", "but not", "other than", "save"
   and "only … not" clause is removed by re-scoping. Either the claim's scope is narrowed so it holds without the
   carve-out, or the excepted case becomes its own claim with its own scope, or it moves to the Rule that owns that
   concern (Rule-5). A list of what a `Y` may never bind to (Rule-2's never-carried acts) is a scope, not an exception,
   and is stated as one.

**The report the Done reads.**
- The claim table: claim, attack, result, survivor.
- The exception count, on main's Rule-3 and on the new one. The new one must read 0.
- An independent attack pass by audit (two-models: the attacker is not the author). Its refutations are fixed or
  answered before the Done.

**Who.**
- counsel: re-scopes and records, on #670 (one writer for Rule-3).
- audit: attacks the claims independently and reports to counsel and the home.
- orchestrate: checks the table, the counts and that each claim in the Rule has a survivor.
- #584's criterion stays on #670 and closes with #585. #605 is asked on its own.

**Execution detail, no new plan-`Y`.** The mutation is the same Rule-3 refactor the plan-`Y` authorized. The
operator's definition says how its output is judged, and meeting it is rewriting inside that refactor.
