## Revision 2 (2026-11-06, provenance #615 entry 2)
Your words: "“Stale” is only for premise that is no longer true on main. There is no aging factor. Re-label “stale”
to something more suitable."
- The class `stale` becomes **false premise** (`false-premise`): a portion that assumes something not true on
  `main` when the reading is made — the work already there, the thing it names removed or changed, or a claim
  about the system that does not hold. It is read against `main`, never against a date or an age.
- The aging reading of revision 1 (a backlog row reopened after `hygiene-stale-days`) is dropped. No input of the
  criterion is a time.
- Why this label: it names the test (is the premise true on `main`?) and not a time. It is the phrase the incident
  log already uses (#81). It covers a premise that was never true as well as one that no longer is.
- The row's title keeps "stale" as written, since titles are immutable (Rule-16). The record and every Rule
  sentence use the new label. Revision 1 is at `f8a59a4`.

## Revision 3 (2026-11-06, provenance #615 entry 3)
Your words: "We’ve managed, albeit less efficiently, without intent-Y, so it should be an optional preference with a
default being engaged. This means that most operators would benefit from three Y, while only the most masterful
would benefit from the two Y"
- A new preference, **`intent-reading`**: `on` | `off`. The template value, and the reading of an absent key, is
  `on`.
  - `on`: three `Y` (intent, plan, done). The intent-`Y` accepts the definition of done.
  - `off`: two `Y`, today's machine unchanged. The plan-`Y` accepts the definition of done, and no intent question
    is asked.
  - It is a form preference, as `merge-disposition` is: it changes how many questions are asked and authorizes
    nothing. Its bullet quotes your words above.
- This instance's own row, `intent-reading | on`, is a preferences-zone PR, so it is asked as its own question
  (Rule-2: a preference value is never carried by a plan-`Y`). It stacks on the agent-zone PR, which carries the
  template key and the Rules that read it.
  - `order: required`: the agent-zone PR merges first. An instance key ahead of its template and surface entry
    failed the push gate once already (#590's preferences PR, `test_surface` S3).
  - Until the row lands, the code reads the default `on` and the preferences guard warns `missing_keys`. Nothing
    else depends on the row.
- The counter of revision 2 (the agent asks only when it judges the reading unclear) is replaced. Who skips the
  intent question is now yours to set, never the agent's judgment.

Ambiguous, with the reading taken:
- **A1. Every row, or only unclear ones?** Read: every row a prompt opens or reopens from `backlog` while
  `intent-reading` is `on`; under `off`, none (revision 3).
- **A2. How the operator declines to continue.** Read: a bare `N` to the intent question archives the row, and an
  `N` with text amends the reading.
- **A3. A premise not true on `main`.** Read: a portion is a false premise when what it assumes does not hold on
  `main` at the reading; age plays no part (revision 2).

False premise or duplicate: none. No live row holds this work. The closest rows are #574, #507, #290 and #34, all done.
Term clash: "intaking". The vocabulary's `intake` already means work proposed by another agent's message (Rule-3
Intake). The step is therefore named **intent reading**, and its answer the **intent-Y**.

## Definition of done (proposed)
The operator's words (provenance #615, entry 1) ask for a formal criterion, the third `Y` and an audit. Proposed, for
the plan-`Y` to accept:
1. A record `dyad/dmc/intent-reading.jsonld`, with `agent/dmc` clean. It classifies each portion of a prompt as
   new, overlap (duplicate when the overlap is complete), supersede or adjacent. The comparison runs against the
   capabilities (the surface, play-books and Rules) and the rows (live, backlog and done). It also marks portions
   that are ambiguous or rest on a false premise, and gives each outcome's consequence.
2. Rule-3, Rule-15 and Rule-16, the d-work play-book and the vocabulary state the intent question, under the
   preference `intent-reading` (`on` by default, `off` the two-`Y` machine; the template row and its bullet):
   - It is the first question on a row a prompt opens or reopens, asked before any plan is drafted.
   - `Y` accepts the reading and the definition of done.
   - An `N` with text amends the reading.
   - A bare `N` archives the row.
3. Mechanisms:
   - `dyad dwork intent <id>` prints the candidate rows and surface items.
   - Under `on`, `dyad plan check` warns on a prompt-opened row planned without an intent-`Y`.
   - The board has an `intent-asked` phase.
4. The audit is filed: `agent-corpus/audits/2026-11-06-intent-and-scope.md`.
5. Delivery:
   - Agent-zone PRs, each with `dyad check --pr` clean and evidence fail 0.
   - `dyad/VERSION` at 1.1.0: a minor, since nothing refuses what it allowed (#611 Q1, Q3).
