# Plan #473 — batch and group verification to cut set-up and tear-down

Base commit of main: d36b1bd

## Intent as read
The Operator: reduce time to completion and tokens during verification, above all evidence collection, by batching and
grouping the known steps so set-ups and tear-downs are not repeated.

## Finding (read-only sub-agent; package.py, hooks/pre-push, land.py, release.py, 147 traces)
- 230 full-suite runs, 21,189 s of foreground suite time; evidence ~146 s cold (core root ~134 s), ~270 lines (~5–6k tokens)
  read and pasted per run; 79 evidence runs for 29 merges (2.72×) in the 09-30 audit.
- The pre-push suite re-runs after evidence on the same tree whenever the memo key misses — it includes a tags digest and the
  UTC date (package.py:372, :380), so a `fetch --tags` between evidence and push silently costs ~146 s, with no assurance gained.
- Shallow/tagless seat clones turn `infra/bundle` and `plan.base->commit` into false FAILs; nothing diagnoses them.
- Four of five CI workflows repeat guards `dyad-package`'s `check` already runs; release archives are built three times.
