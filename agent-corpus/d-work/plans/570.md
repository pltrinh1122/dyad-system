## Revision 3 (2026-11-04): the altitude criterion, with #570–#573 as its exemplar
This replaces revision 2's question. The operator framed the tuning: you disposition **one parent row**, and its
`Y` is carried to the children, or you disposition **each child row**. Today the choice comes from the agent's own
grouping of work into blocks, with a parent added whenever there is more than one block.

**Attacks on the frame:**
- **C1: "carried-Y is a new mechanism."** **Refuted as to existence, confirmed as to criterion.** The mechanism is
  Rule-3's mandate (#290): #571–#573 each carry `Y plan (mandate #570)` and `mode: implicit`, and 104 rows already
  carry a mandate entry. What is new is a criterion for when to use it. Today none exists; revision 1's A7 grouped by
  the agent's own blocks.
- **C2: "the choice is the number of questions."** **Refuted.** Under batching, three children cost about as many
  questions as one parent (revision 2, B1). What differs is what you **verify**:
  - one Done over the delivered set, read through the parent's evidence;
  - or a Done per child, each on its own evidence.

  Altitude is the granularity of your verification. Batching already sets the question count.
- **C3: "the agent's grouping is a neutral input."** **Refuted.** The same work splits into one block or five
  depending on who draws the lines. A criterion fed by the agent's blocks is the agent's choice wearing a criterion.
  The inputs have to be properties of the work that the record shows, not of how the agent cut it.

**Draft criterion** (`dyad/dmc/altitude.jsonld`; you set the default and the switches):

| # | input, read from the work | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | a child's act is never carried under Rule-2: a preference value, a release, a destructive act, an Intake | that child is a **row you disposition**, whatever the default |
| L2 | children must land together, or one's value depends on another's | **carried** under the parent |
| L3 | the children's number is unknown when the plan is asked (discovered while working) | **carried** (accretion) |
| L4 | otherwise | your default, preference `altitude`: `parent` carries every child; `child` makes each a row you disposition |
