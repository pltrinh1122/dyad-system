**The method proposed: diverge by lens, converge by cause.** It is drawn from three established practices, each
doing one job: a jobs-to-be-done statement anchors, a fishbone diagram's categories diverge, and the five whys
converge. The steps:
1. **Anchor.** Write the segment's job against the root: "When I let an agent `<act>` on `<what I answer for>`, I want
   to trust it enough to answer for the result, so that `<the stake>`." Every later statement is a reason this fails.
2. **Diverge, by lens.** Generate statements without judging them, at least two per lens. The ten lenses of trust:
   visibility (can I see what it did), verifiability (can I check it is right), predictability (will it do what it
   said), reversibility (can I undo it), attribution (can I show who decided), competence (does it know this domain),
   intent (did it understand what I meant), attention (do I have time to check), consequence (what breaks if it is
   wrong), and accountability to others (who will hold me to it). Your symptom is one statement, under attention.
3. **Cluster.** Group the statements by what they share. Each cluster is a candidate cause.
4. **Converge, by asking why.** For each cluster, ask why it happens until the answer is either the root (the anchor
   fails) or something the segment could remove. That last answer is a **root cause**.
5. **Test each root cause.** Keep it only if removing it would remove most of its cluster's symptoms, and it is not a
   missing feature (`solution-shaped`).
6. **Select.** One to three root causes become the problem section's entries, ranked. Their symptoms stay as the
   reader's words for the pitch. Every line is labelled H until a reader confirms it.

**One record for step 5**, `collateral-problem-depth`: "Is this problem entry a symptom, a cause or a root cause?"
Outcomes, in order:
- `symptom`: something the segment observes;
- `cause`: something that explains symptoms but is itself explained by a deeper answer;
- `root-cause`: removing it removes most of its symptoms, and the next why reaches the anchor;
- `solution-shaped`: it names a missing feature, which the problem record already fails.
