## 5. Dialectic
**Thesis: plain "Disposition".** Exact, shared by both repos (#142 §2), with no collision. Its flaw
(#148 N2) is that everyday English reads "disposition" as temperament.

**Antithesis: a sourced name teaches the invariant through a story people already know.** But the
test above shows the risk. Most myths and fictions *contradict* the architecture on closer reading:
- Themis: an oracle, against no-oracle.
- Dioscuri: a meld.
- Golem: a runaway servant.
- Jeeves: disposal by manipulation.
- Royal Assent: a rubber stamp.

A name imports its whole story, including the parts that fail.

**Synthesis: take the name from *practice*, not myth, where the procedure itself is the invariant.**
**Placet** survives N6 best of all sourced names. Its real, recorded voting practice has a proposer
separate from the disposers, a closed set of answers that matches the architecture's grammar
(yes / no / yes-with-amendments), and a written record of each vote. It is not a metaphor for
disposition; it *is* one. What it leaves out, delegation (implicit mode), is exactly what
**Mission Command** covers, and that doctrine is legible to client organizations.

## 6. Recommendation
- **Architecture name: Placet Architecture**, glossed once as "every consequential act carries a
  recorded *placet*: yes, no, or yes-with-amendments, given by a human per turn, in advance or
  beforehand". It keeps "Disposition" as the *technical term* inside, because both repos already
  use it.
- **Client framing: Mission Command** for the implicit mode ("your people set intent, agents act
  within it, and anything outside it comes back to them"). It is used as a description, not a name.
- **Runner-up name: Ma'at**, if a mythic name is wanted: the only myth tested whose roles (check,
  record, judge) map without contradiction. Its cost is cultural weight and legibility.

**Concessions:** Placet's Latin and ecclesiastical origin will need its one-line gloss wherever a
client first meets it. The *(gk)* facts above, including the Vatican II ballot forms and the
Royal Assent date, should be verified before external use, and no trademark or product search was
run.
