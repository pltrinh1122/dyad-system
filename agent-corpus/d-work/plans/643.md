**Survivor: one clause, three classes.** Rule-3 Plan's execution-detail list gains **internal realization**, a
change whose effect stays inside the PR. It takes one of three forms:
- **placement:** the six conditions of the intent reading;
- **implied means:** the plan's text names the effect, and the means adds no public surface;
- **compatible extension:** an optional parameter of an internal function, defaulting to the old behaviour, with no
  caller changed and a test showing it.

Public surface means a `dyadlib.CLI` row, a `dyad/surface.md` row, a released craft's path, or Rule or preference
text. Each use is named in the completion evidence as a departure line.
