**Ambiguous, with the reading taken:**
- **A1. Where the set lives, and what landing it means.** The desk's charter writes `site/` (infra zone). Every
  tracked path in this repository is visible to anyone who can read it, so landing the canvas on `main` publishes it
  in effect, while §5 says a canvas is accepted, never published. Read: **the set lives at `site/lean-canvas/`**, one
  file per section (`problem.md` … `unfair-advantage.md`) and `index.md` (the labels, the riskiest assumption and the
  fit verdict). It stays on its branch and in its PR until your done-`Y`. That `Y` both accepts it as the foundation
  and lets it land, visibly. If you want it accepted but never visible, say so and the plan keeps it off `main`.
- **A2. Who the product is for.** The record names "an Operator who delegates building and operating work to an AI
  agent and stays accountable for the result", which #754 found `too-broad`. Read, every word a **hypothesis**:
  - **segment:** a technical person who already runs a CLI coding agent (Claude Code or another) on repositories they
    answer for;
  - **early adopters:** those among them who today review every agent change by hand before it lands, because they
    have been burned by one they didn't.
  You may amend the segment. Every other section follows from it.
- **A3. Evidence versus hypothesis.** The repo proves what the system does, and that one operator uses it daily. It
  proves nothing about any other reader. Read: an entry is **evidence** only when it cites a path or count about the
  system itself (the solution, its costs, its metrics as counted). Everything said about the segment, its words, its
  channels or its willingness to pay is a **hypothesis**. The riskiest assumption, named in `index.md`, is that the
  early adopters feel the review burden strongly enough to accept a `Y` on every plan and every Done.
- **A4. Revenue.** No record shows a price or a sale. Read: "none, and why" — free and open, offered to practitioners.
  This passes revenue's `empty` honestly; it is still a hypothesis that this is the intended model.
- **A5. The fence.** No person, client, employer or private repository appears in any piece. The operator appears
  only as "the operator", the role.

**Definition of done (proposed).**
- Ten files under `site/lean-canvas/`: nine sections, each named after its record, and `index.md`. Every entry is
  labelled **H** or **E**, each **E** citing its path or count.
- Each section judged by its record and the canvas by the gate and the fit record. The aim is `ready` throughout, with
  any section that cannot honestly pass reported as it stands, never dressed up to pass.
- One infra-zone PR on its branch, not landed before your done-`Y`, which accepts the canvas as the foundation.
- Nothing posted or sent outside the repository.
