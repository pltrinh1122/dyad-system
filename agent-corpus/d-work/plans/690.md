### The play-book, as it will read

# Play-book: judgement trace (core craft, `dyad-operator`; #690)

How the agent examines one of its own acts that the operator questions: from the symptom, back through every Rule sentence, decision record and preference that decided it, to its cause. Its first product is clarity, the operator's model and the agent's reading made to agree; a change is a possible second product, never a required one. The worked example is plan #689, revisions 1–3.

**Trigger.** The operator writes "judgement trace: <the behavior>", or asks why the agent did something and asks for it traced. Never started by the agent on its own act, which is an incident (Rule-3 Incidents), nor on another unit's act without the operator's prompt.

**Steps** (`dyad/runbooks/judgement-trace.md`, inference throughout; no command decides a step):
- T1 Symptom: the act, quoted from the reply or the record where it happened, with its row and commit.
- T2 Trace back: each decision that led to the act, one line each, newest first, every one cited by path and line as it reads on `main` now, never from memory or from the agent's earlier summary. A line the agent cannot cite is written `uncited` and is a finding of its own.
- T3 Conflicts: every pair of cited texts that decide the same case differently, and every cited text that no longer matches the code or the store on `main`.
- T4 Cause: each line of T2 and each finding of T3 classed by `dmc:judgement-cause` (below), and the trace's cause stated: the highest class by the record's precedence.
- T5 Outcome: what the class says, and nothing more.
- T6 Report: one reply, in this order: the symptom, the trace, the conflicts, the cause, the outcome; and **corrections**, every earlier statement of the agent's that the trace overturns, named as a correction with what it said (#689 revision 3's "corrections" is the form).

**Defaults.** A trace that ends `intended` or `undetermined` writes nothing and opens no row: it is a read-only answer (Rule-3 Scope), so the examination costs the operator no question. A trace whose outcome opens or revises a row is stored in that row's plan file, as #689's revisions were, so the record of why survives the chat.

**Conduct.** The class is the agent's reading, presented to the operator, never declared final (Rule-2: an agent-authored verdict is the operator's to finalise). Two fences on that reading, from the craft invariants: anti-cave, the agent never classes its act `misapplied` because the operator questioned it, only because a cited text decided otherwise; and two-models, it never classes an act `intended` to defend it, only because every cited text agrees with the act. Where the operator's model and the trace differ and the trace is `intended`, the trace says so plainly: that is the clarity the play-book exists for.

### The decision record, `dmc:judgement-cause`

Decides: for one act the operator questioned, traced to the texts that decided it, what caused it, and what follows.

Inputs: the act (T1); the cited texts that decided it (T2); the conflicts and stale texts found (T3); whether each cited text, read on `main`, decides the act's case.

| class | when | then | effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| `misapplied` | a cited text on `main` decides the case, and the act differs from what it decides (#687: `dmc:intent-reading` said `duplicate` → nothing done, the agent recommended `Y`) | an incident (Rule-3 Incidents), recorded in the row where the act happened while it is not `done`, else in a row of its own; the act corrected where it still can be; the text unchanged unless another class also holds | `acts` |
| `conflict` | two cited texts decide the case differently, so any act breaks one (#689 T4: the Guide's "always a registration row" against Rule-3's "monitoring opens no row") | a proposed change to the text that yields, by the precedence Rule-5 and Rule-6 give (a Rule over a record over a play-book over a guide), asked as a row's intent question; Rule-5's gap noted | `asks` |
| `stale` | a cited text no longer matches the code or the store on `main` (#689 T4: "a bare touch claims every open row", untrue since #567) | a restore by `dmc:blocker-home` (a restore rides a carried row or an open row's PR; a change is its own row) | `asks` |
| `gap` | no cited text decides the case, so the agent's act was its own inference (#689 T3: `duplicate` said nothing of a row already open) | a proposed addition to the record or Rule nearest the case, asked as a row's intent question | `asks` |
| `intended` | every cited text decides the case and the act matches them all | the explanation alone: no row, no change; the operator's model is what moves, unless the operator prompts a change, which opens a row as any prompt does | `settles` |
| `undetermined` | a step cannot be cited or read: the record is gone, the transcript is not on this machine, the act was another unit's | the missing input named and the operator asked for it; no class is guessed | `asks` |

Precedence: classes are given per line of T2 and per finding of T3; the trace's cause is the highest present: `misapplied`, then `conflict`, then `stale`, then `gap`, then `undetermined`, then `intended`. Several may hold at once, and every one present gets its outcome: #689 held `misapplied`, `conflict`, `stale` and `gap`.
Default: `undetermined`, since a class guessed is the error this record exists to catch.
Judged by: inference (`dyad:kind` inference), the agent's reading; a precedent never settles `misapplied` (`acts`) and may settle only `intended` (`settles`) and the three that ask (`dmc:precedent-scope`).
Home: `playbooks/judgement-trace.md`; also in `rules/RULE-3-d-work.md` (Incidents, by reference), `playbooks/d-work.md` (blocker home).
