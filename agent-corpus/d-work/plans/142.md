## 2. Disambiguation of overlapping terms

Relation key: **same** (same concept, compatible), **overlap** (shared core, different scope),
**collision** (same word, different thing), **inverted** (same word, opposing commitments).

Two are ratified: decision-making (gates G1–G6, five disposition modes, DecisionRecords) and dsys-mutation (rung ladder). ## 5. What would make a scoped version of the claim true

These are observations, not proposals; each would be its own d-work under Rule-3 and Rule-9.
- **As a library, never as kernel.** Rule-14 p2 fixes the kernel as minimal and enumerated. The

  `manifest.md`). Being replaceable is the opposite of being a kernel.

  worked example, but its entities do not map (§3 rows 1–2). (b) dyad-system has no zero-inference
  executor (component 8). dsys's executor is the only candidate in reach, but adopting it would put
  run-books out of the Agent's reach, which contradicts Rule-19's "run by either party".
- **Terminology first.** Any integration would first need the collisions in §2 resolved. At minimum:
  run-book (inverted), harness, kernel, the `dyad` CLI word (inverted), role and event.
