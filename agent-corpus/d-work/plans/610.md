**Reading proposed:** 2.0.0 is the planned major in which the system moves from one disposer to several. Its breaks
are the Rule, vocabulary and record changes that name which operator disposes what. Additive multi-operator pieces
(cabinets, routing, presence) may land in 1.x as opt-in preparation, each break's deprecation among them. 1.x
otherwise scales single-operator mode. The candidate list is re-sorted by this theme in the next revision, and the
operator ratifies the list and order at #610's Done.
**Design constraint (added 2026-11-05 on the operator's request for a decision criterion):** a single-operator system
upgrades to 2.0.0 by a mechanical port step, with its one operator named by default so its records and Rules read as
before. No system then needs a 1.x after 2.0.0. The criteria behind it: the upgrade path, who reads the number, how long
1.x would live after 2.0, the cost of each fix on two lines, and the operator's attention. Only if some system cannot
take that step does keeping 1.x alongside 2.x (amending #611) earn its cost.
**Counter:** #611's Q2 and Q5 could be amended, so a major marks a theme and 1.x is maintained beside 2.x. **Answer:**
that needs a maintenance branch the release path, the automaton and the sweep do not support (E4), and a major that
breaks nothing tells an installing system nothing. Amending #611 is the operator's call, as its own row.
