# Plan #584 — the message-route criterion: post to the inbox and ring the doorbell by default; poll only where no direct message reaches

Base commit of main: 891435d

## Definition of done (proposed; amend with N and text)
A report you can read in one pass:
1. `dyad/dmc/message-route.jsonld`: for each case, whether the inbox, the doorbell, or polling.
2. The Rule-3 sentence that makes the doorbell the default after an actionable post.
3. The list of units that poll and why, empty today: every live unit is a cloud session that receives direct messages.
Nothing in it depends on anything after the merge. Whether W1 (unread past 4 h) stays at 0 is the sweep's to watch.

## Intent as read
Codify when a message goes by inbox, by direct message, or both. The doorbell is rung by default, which removes
polling. A unit is set up to poll its inbox only where a direct message can't reach it.

## Falsification
| # | claim | attack | result | survivor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | A doorbell can replace the inbox | A direct message is addressed to one session id: lost on a hop, unreceivable by a terminal desk, and unsendable by the automaton (Rule-3, Rule-16, #536). | **refuted** | Both, every time: the inbox carries the content and the doorbell wakes. The DMC states the pair as the default. |
| M2 | "By default" leaves no case without a doorbell | Three senders cannot ring: the automaton (no session); a unit whose receiver has no chat (a terminal desk); a sender whose receiver is itself. | **survives, scoped** | Automaton notices go to the home, and the home rings the unit they concern (the play-book's W1 line already says so). A terminal receiver polls (next row). Self-addressed posts need no doorbell. |
| M3 | Polling is a choice | A unit that cannot receive a direct message has no other way to hear. | **survives** | Such a unit is set up to poll: `dyad inbox read` at each turn start, as today, plus a scheduled wake where its host offers one. The DMC names the condition (no direct message reaches), never a preference. |
| M4 | The CLI can ring for you | `dyad inbox post` is the kernel CLI; ringing is the harness's `send_message`, which no CLI command can call. | **confirmed against a wrapper** | The pair is conduct, written as the Rule-3 sentence. W1 (#569) is its backstop. #572's inference map already lists the doorbell as needing a session. |
| M5 | A cc copy needs a doorbell | Orchestrate's 10 unread copies sat 13 h (2026-11-04). | **confirmed** | A copy the receiver must act on is actionable and is rung. A copy for information only is marked `--kind notice`, rung at the receiver's next relay. |
