Proposed `dmc:re-present`, for a question the board records as asked and not answered (precedence top first):
- `void`: a head the question names has moved, or a row it names went terminal or was withdrawn. The question is taken back (the automaton's `head-moved` and `withdrawn`, Rule-16) and a new one asked on the new state. Mechanical.
- `full`: the question still binds, but what it cites changed on main since it was asked: the plan file it binds was revised, or a Rule, decision record or play-book the plan or its reading cites changed, or a row it refs changed state. Re-presented in full, what/why/how, counter and reconciliation (Rule-10, `relay-form`), the change named first, the plan revision recorded before it is asked. Mechanical detection (paths and rows the plan cites, against main since the question's `since`); whether the change bears on the decision is the home's inference, and when in doubt it is `full`.
- `remind`: nothing it cites changed, but it was displaced: at least one other question has been the last line of a reply since it was asked, or it is older than `inflight-question-hours` (24, an existing preference). Re-presented once, verbatim with one line of what it decides, as the last line of the next reply that has no question of its own, or batched with others of its kind. Mechanical.
- `pending`: nothing changed and it has not been displaced. It stays in the closing line's count alone. Mechanical.
- One `Y/N` per reply holds (Rule-10): a reply that owes several re-presentations asks the highest by this precedence, or one batch of one kind, and the rest stay counted.

Definition of done (proposed): the record `dyad/dmc/re-present.jsonld`; `dyad board resume`'s owed list printing each owed question's outcome and its reason (the change, the displacement or the age) in place of "re-present each question verbatim, or report it pending"; the d-work play-book's step naming the record; the surface row; a test of each outcome.
