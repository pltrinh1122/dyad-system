user-agent chat service.  V2.0 and on would be for multi-user "forum" group discussions.'
Its context is #701's entries 2–4: the knowledge base and FAQ-feedback agent, growing from users' symptoms, a vehicle
for marketing (credibility by demonstrated knowledge) and research (new symptoms).

its artifacts a PRD, an Architecture and a UX/UI Design; adversarial testing with the operator's verbatim symptom prompts
from this chat; and API-based inference, the provider swappable, the least expensive found, a donation-based
self-funding model later, toward "The Dyad Practice Foundation" as a non-profit.
