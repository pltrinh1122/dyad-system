## Definition of done (proposed; amend with N and text)

1. The relay to orchestrate on #573 lands, with orchestrate added to the board's owners in the same commit (today
   it was refused twice, at 17:2x and 17:4x UTC).
2. A batch whose disposition line is refused (today's `;` in #573's `-d`) lands nothing: the board lines and

## Intent as read
Both errors today were sequencing slips that a command could prevent, not judgment:
- **E1:** `inbox post` refuses a relay to a unit the board entry doesn't name as owner (write-ahead, #552). The
  home then edits the board and posts again. This happened three times on 2026-11-04: #585, #573 and #564's operate.
- **E2:** the home writes the record, the board and the relays as separate commands. A refused record didn't stop
  the relays after it (#573's B3 disposition, 2026-11-04).
