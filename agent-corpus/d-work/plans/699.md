**Falsification of the claim (Rule-9).**
| id | attack | result | survivor |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | one list serves every reader | **survives, scoped** | each symptom carries its proficiency, its way of working and its when; a reader is met by the subset at their proficiency, never the whole list |
| C2 | canonical means fixed | **confirmed** (the attack holds) | it must change as evidence arrives: append-only, a symptom superseded and never edited, its id never reused; entry and exit by a record, never by hand |
| C3 | symptoms engage better than problems | **survives** (H) | a reader recognises what they see, not a root cause; plainness's `unobservable` already restates causes as what is seen. Hypothesis until readers are tested |
| C4 | the symptoms are the nine classes | **refuted** | a class groups failures; a symptom is the reader's own sentence, several per class (V5 "it gives way when I push back" is a facet of O1) |
| C5 | a hand-kept list drifts from the trees | **confirmed** | so the list is a **projection**: every top-level symptom of every source tree and reference tree, deduplicated to one canonical wording, each entry naming its sources (`⟵ tree:id`); never edited by hand |
| C6 | an empathy list invites emotional overclaim | **survives, scoped** | the craft's "emotion is a claim" (§3) binds it: each entry H until a reader's evidence, and a pitch cites the entry's id |
| C7 | "user" is too broad: the reader may not use agents yet | **survives, scoped** | the list names what a person working with agents sees; a reader who does not use agents is reached by the problems, not this list |

**Definition of done (proposed):**
- A record, `collateral-symptom-canon` (chronicle), deciding whether a symptom enters the list, and as what: it must be
  observable, plain at its proficiency (by `collateral-problem-plainness`), distinct (no restatement of another entry or
  of a cause), rooted (a class and a tree node) and sourced (a source or reference tree, or a reader's evidence).
  Outcomes: restated, cause, unrooted, unsourced, duplicate (joins an entry, adding its source), canon.

  wording, class, proficiency, way of working, when, and sources; append-only by supersession.
