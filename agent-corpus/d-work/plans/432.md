## Survivor — the cos's minimum (five acts, all short, all ledger-only or chat)
1. **Receive**: record every Operator prompt and disposition verbatim (row + provenance, one ledger-only
   commit, seconds), and open the row it names.
2. **Route**: dispatch each new row to the secretary whose charter covers it, with the Operator's words
   verbatim (R29) — counsel for falsifications, Rule and vocabulary text; engineer for mechanism; distribute
   for releases and ports; audit for findings and hardening; operate for the host.
3. **Present**: one question per reply, WHAT/WHY/HOW (#424), the seat's text as evidence; a preference as its
   own question (#427, presented not pushed).
4. **Relay back**: each disposition to its seat verbatim; the seat executes (record, merge, close).
5. **Board**: read and report the board of programs; keep the check-in armed.
Delegated, never done at the cos: plan drafting (a fork or the owning secretary writes the plan file; the
cos asks the Y), falsification analyses (counsel), evidence runs and merges (the owning seat), branch pushes
and PRs of any zone (the owning seat; preferences included, drafted by the secretary whose row needs it),
incident rows (the seat whose act it was), release cutting (distribute), board rendering and head
verification (the cos's fork, #425). Availability rule: the cos's checkout runs nothing longer than a
ledger-only push; anything that runs the suite runs in a seat or in a worktree.
