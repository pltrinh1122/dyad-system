## Definition of done
The operator's words, provenance #570 (the prompt behind revision 4), for the attention criterion:

> a pair of 'Y' that can produce the largest scope of work where i'm able to evaluate

The report that answers it:
1. The DoD step is in the d-work play-book (planning), and the plan form has a Definition of done section.
2. The completion evidence of every row planned after this lands opens with that section, quoted, and the report that
   answers it. This row's own completion is the first.
3. The first use is the home's backlog report on the 62 backlog rows (#570 revision 4, V1–V3): the counts of rows in,
   DoDs out, absorbed and split, and how many of the proposed DoDs you amended.
4. The measure, your messages per delivered row, is reported from the trace (A2t, folded into this row).

Item 3 is the home's draft and item 4 is the trace's; counsel delivers items 1 and 2.

## Intent as read
Revision 4 of #570 replaces work-shaped partitioning with DoD-shaped partitioning:
- Each definition of done that you can evaluate is one row, carrying one plan-`Y` and one done-`Y` over the largest
  scope that DoD lets you judge.
- An unfamiliar domain draws finer DoDs, and so smaller rows.

The mechanism has three parts:
- a **step** in the play-book's planning, which elicits or proposes the DoD and partitions by it;
- a **section** in the plan form;
- the done's **evidence opening** with that section.
