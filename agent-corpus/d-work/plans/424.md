# Plan #424 — relay form: WHAT / WHY / HOW at the Operator's altitude

## Intent as read
The Operator: a relay is not passing on a secretary's words (those are citation and evidence); the cos meets
the Operator at the Operator's altitude and presents each disposition as WHAT the matter is, WHY it matters,
HOW it is being addressed. Configurable: a preference for how the cos presents a disposition.
