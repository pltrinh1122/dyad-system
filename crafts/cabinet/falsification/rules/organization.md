cos-board, authority granted by content-scoped mandates, every pair sharing the same ledgers — is codified as the
default organizational preference, with an Operating Guide for Operators. The nine attacks on whether a `cos`
craft overlaps another craft's domain are plan #369 revision 4's (survivor: three cuts — Rule-8/sysadmin's,
Rule-19's, syseng's); the attacks below are on the rule's content, run while writing it against the first live
program (#369) rather than against the plan.

| # | Attack | Result | Survivor |
|---|--------|--------|----------|
| 1 | **The rule binds the Agent's process** (Rule-4 Boundaries: a rule that binds the process is an Agent Rule and belongs in the core; `check_tended` warns on the tokens). | **Refuted at drafting; the classification stays inference** | The craft guard's agent-token scan reports no token after drafting (`the Agent`, `counter-prompt`, `Y/N:`, `ratif`, `plan-Y`, `Done-Y` absent from `rules/*.md`); what a row is, who disposes, what a mandate binds and what may never be accreted are cited to Rules 2, 3, 7, 15, 16 and never restated — the Rule-8/sysadmin cut (#369 revision 4, attack 1).
