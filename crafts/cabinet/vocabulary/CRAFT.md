# cabinet craft vocabulary (Tended terms)

One row per term; `rule` names the craft rule (`../rules/<rule>.md`) that owns it. A craft term is referenced,
never defined, by the agent vocabulary (`dyad/vocabulary/VOCABULARY.md`, Rule-6 Boundaries), as `cabinet:<term>`;
no agent term is redefined here — `seat`, `holder`, `presence file`, `mandate`, `mandate scope`, `accreted
row`, `mode`, `escalation` and `unit name` are the core's, and `secretary` and `cos-board`, which entered the core with
#369 C7, are this craft's again since #560 (only a multi-unit organization has them) — so this table holds only what
the organization adds. Checked by the craft guard (`dyad craft check cabinet`).

| term | definition | rule |
|------|------------|------|
| charter | one row of the mandates table: a unit's standing mandate, content-scoped (#253 R23–R26) — the function it serves, its domain (what it owns), its fence (the paths and acts its rows may touch), its ceiling (how many rows it may accrete under one plan), its escalations (what returns to the operator) and its revocation (the preferences-zone change that ends it); held by the operator, read by the unit | organization |
| cos | the title of the home (the core's term, #553): the unit beside the operator that keeps the board of programs — every parent row and its children across the member units — relays the operator's words to the units verbatim, writes no row for a unit and answers no unit's question; one per operator, named `program-<hp>` (`program-<operator>-<hp>` in a multi-operator tree) | organization |
| desk | a unit on a secretary's board that holds one topic of that function's domain under the member's charter, named `<function>-<topic>-<hp>`; it keeps no board of its own, and a secretary opens as many as its concurrent work needs (#416) | organization |
| roll-up | the one line per unit the home reads at every reply while a program runs — children held, open questions, the oldest and the stale — folded to at most seven lines for one reader: a channel limit, never the span of control, which counts dyad units (organization §1); never the units' own evidence restated | organization |
| function | the first token of a unit name and the first column of the mandates table: what the operator watches on that unit's board — `program`, `counsel`, `audit`, `engineer`, `distribute`, `operate`, `communicate`, `orchestrate` in the default organization; a verb for a flow, a noun only for the portfolio | naming |
| topic | the optional middle token of a unit name: the one subject a desk holds under its function (`engineer-ledgerd-<hp>`), or, for `program`, the operator's handle in a multi-operator tree | naming |
| secretary | a unit beside the home that keeps a cos-board over one function's domain under a charter (a row of the mandates table), named `<function>-<hp>`; one line, or part of a folded line, of the home's roll-up; a solo unless the operator pairs it; it opens desks, units of one topic each, as its concurrent work needs (#368, #369, #416, #417; defined here since #560) | organization |
| cos-board | the board a unit with a function keeps over its scope and reads at every reply: the rows of its charter's domain and the units under it; the home's is the board of programs, a secretary's the board of its function and its desks; its form and reading are the Operating Guide's (#369; defined here since #560) | organization |
