# cos craft vocabulary (Tended terms)

One row per term; `rule` names the craft rule (`../rules/<rule>.md`) that owns it. A craft term is referenced,
never defined, by the Agent vocabulary (`dyad/vocabulary/VOCABULARY.md`, Rule-6 Boundaries), as `cos:<term>`;
no Agent term is redefined here — `seat`, `holder`, `presence file`, `mandate`, `mandate scope`, `accreted
row`, `mode`, `escalation` are the core's, and `cos-board`, `member-cos` and `seat name` enter the core with
#369 C7 — so this table holds only what the organization adds. Checked by the craft guard (`dyad craft check cos`).

| term | definition | rule |
|------|------------|------|
| charter | one row of the mandates table: a seat's standing mandate, content-scoped (#253 R23–R26) — the function it serves, its domain (what it owns), its fence (the paths and acts its rows may touch), its ceiling (how many rows it may accrete under one plan), its escalations (what returns to the Operator) and its revocation (the preferences-zone change that ends it); held by the Operator, read by the seat | organization |
| leader-cos | the seat beside the Operator that keeps the board of programs — every parent row and its children across the member seats — relays the Operator's words to the seats verbatim, writes no row for a seat and answers no seat's question; one per Operator, named `program-<hp>` (`program-<operator>-<hp>` in a multi-Operator tree) | organization |
| member-cabinet | a seat on a member-cos's board that holds one topic of that function's domain under the member's charter, named `<function>-<topic>-<hp>`; it keeps no board of its own unless it is itself a cos | organization |
| roll-up | the one line per seat the leader reads at every reply while a program runs — children held, open questions, the oldest and the stale — the span of control's unit: at most seven lines for one reader, never the seats' own evidence restated | organization |
| function | the first token of a seat name and the first column of the mandates table: what the Operator watches on that seat's board — `program`, `counsel`, `audit`, `engineer`, `distribute`, `operate` in the default organization; a verb for a flow, a noun only for the portfolio | naming |
| topic | the optional middle token of a seat name: the one subject a member-cabinet holds under its function (`engineer-ledgerd-<hp>`), or, for `program`, the Operator's handle in a multi-Operator tree | naming |
