# Falsification record — "a decision record can tell a good product-marketing pitch from a bad one, and seed a family for other collateral" (`../../rules/collateral.md`; dyad-system #668)

**Claim (Operator's, d-work #668):** "i need to be able to discern a good product marketing pitch. develop the `dmc`
for it", with a set of such records to follow for other collateral (a lean canvas, a product vision board, their
elements), and a pitch judged on "the emotional elements that connects and attracts the reader" as well as on truth.
The rule as written is plan #668 revision 2's survivor.

| # | Attack | Result | Survivor |
|---|--------|--------|----------|
| 1 | **The gate does not fit every kind**: a lean canvas box is a fragment, so `unanchored` cannot ask it for a citation. | **Survives, scoped** | A canvas claim ("customers pay for X") is still a claim and still needs its path, count or test; a box that only names a segment makes no claim, so the test passes it. |
| 2 | **The split is premature**: one record is enough until a second kind arrives. | **Survives, scoped** | The Operator named the second and third kinds, and the gate restates rules that already bind every chronicle form (§1, the fence), so they already recur. **Verdict:** the claim **survives**, scoped: the records decide what a piece needs; whether a reader is moved stays
inference, read by the Operator at publication.

**Limits.** No pitch has yet been judged by these records in production; the worked judgement in d-work #668's PR body is
the first. The family's later records are named, not written.
