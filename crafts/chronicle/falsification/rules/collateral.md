# Falsification record — "a decision record can tell a good product-marketing pitch from a bad one, and seed a family for other collateral" (`../../rules/collateral.md`; dyad-system #668)

**Claim (Operator's, d-work #668):** "i need to be able to discern a good product marketing pitch. develop the `dmc`
for it", with a set of such records to follow for other collateral (a lean canvas, a product vision board, their
elements), and a pitch judged on "the emotional elements that connects and attracts the reader" as well as on truth.
The rule as written is plan #668 revision 2's survivor.

| # | Attack | Result | Survivor |
|---|--------|--------|----------|
| 1 | **The gate does not fit every kind**: a lean canvas box is a fragment, so `unanchored` cannot ask it for a citation. | **Survives, scoped** | A canvas claim ("customers pay for X") is still a claim and still needs its path, count or test; a box that only names a segment makes no claim, so the test passes it. |
| 2 | **The split is premature**: one record is enough until a second kind arrives. | **Survives, scoped** | The Operator named the second and third kinds, and the gate restates rules that already bind every chronicle form (§1, the fence), so they already recur. Moving a test later is one edit to two data files. |
| 3 | **`ready` reads as permission to publish.** | **Refuted** | Its consequence is the publication question, the Operator's alone (`chronicle.md` §6); the record decides nothing about the world. |
| 4 | **`invented` needs a reference for what the system holds.** | **Survives** | It reads the system's own statement of purpose and its practice, named in its inputs. |
| 5 | **Truth alone leaves the reader cold** (the Operator's amend). | **Confirmed, the rule extended** | The pitch record adds `cold`, `abstract` and `no-pull`: the felt problem in the reader's terms, a concrete moment, and what changes for them with a next step. |
| 6 | **An emotional appeal cannot earn its place**: a named feeling is a claim no path proves, so `unanchored` would strike it. | **Survives, scoped** | A feeling is a claim about the reader, anchored to the stated problem or the brief's reader (§3); `manufactured` strikes what goes beyond them. |
| 7 | **The emotional tests cannot be judged**: two readers disagree whether a pitch is `cold`. | **Survives, scoped** | Judged by inference, stated; the Operator reads every `ready` pitch anyway, and agreed rulings may become precedents. |
| 8 | **`abstract` duplicates `decorated`.** | **Refuted** | `decorated` removes ornament that adds nothing; `abstract` asks for a concrete moment that is missing. |
| 9 | **The emotional tests belong in the gate**, since a vision board also has to connect. | **Survives, scoped** | A lean canvas is a working tool whose job is clarity; `cold` would fail every box. A kind that needs feeling takes the pitch's outcome names (§4). |

**Verdict:** the claim **survives**, scoped: the records decide what a piece needs; whether a reader is moved stays
inference, read by the Operator at publication.

**Limits.** No pitch has yet been judged by these records in production; the worked judgement in d-work #668's PR body is
the first. The family's later records are named, not written.

Disposition: see ledger #668.
